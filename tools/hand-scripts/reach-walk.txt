# Walking with a reached menu open, and switching between containers
# (vr/ReachOpen.h; [Hands] ReachOpens, ReachWalkAwayMetres; the tester,
# 2026-10-09: "es muss möglich sein weiterhin sich normal zu bewegen mit
# stick links bei den neuen offenen menüs, wenn ich zu weit weg gehe
# schließt es sich automatisch. und ich kann zwischen container und deren
# menüs wechseln ... indem ich meine hand dem jeweiligen container
# annähere"). An empty chest (000086C4) is placed at the player's feet
# (PlaceAtMe lands it there, see reach-open.txt), the player walks a metre
# on and a second one is placed at the new feet. The right hand comes down
# onto the second chest: its menu opens. With it up the left stick walks
# the player back the metre - the player's controls run under the menu
# (the gate at 0040DC61) and the menu stays, the feet move - and the hand
# comes down onto the first chest: the switch closes the second's menu and
# opens the first's. Then the player walks on past ReachWalkAwayMetres: the
# menu closes by itself. The console takes its line for some seconds after
# a mark and eats the stick's keys meanwhile, so the walks wait 8 s.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\reach-walk.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=1
ini Hands ReachWalkAwayMetres=1.5
ini Hands WalkDirection=head
ini Look ContainerInWorld=1
console-at ready player.placeatme 000086C4 1 60 0
console-at placed2 player.placeatme 000086C4 1 60 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect reached - activated
expect its menu opened
expect Menu pause: the player's controls run - the sticks walk
expect the player walks with it open
expect a hand at another - closed for it
expect the other one reached - activated
expect walked away - closed
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Reach: the close-all-menus function

wait 10
mark ready
wait 8

left stick 0 0
wait 1

wait 8
