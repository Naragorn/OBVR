# A weapon place's circle shown faintly while the drawn weapon is near it (vr/PlaceNear.h,
# camera/hands/HolsterNear.cpp; [Hands] HolsterNear, [Hands] PlaceHintOpacity; Nadi, 2026-11-16).
# The harness save's Elven Longsword, kept at the left hip, drawn there: the hand is in the hip's place
# with the drawn sword, the hips' circles come ("the places shown"). The hand taken up and away: they go
# ("the places hidden"). The hand, no grip, into the right hip's place: they come again; up and away: they
# go. (The first run, 2026-11-16, equipped an Iron Longsword by the console: the save's sword keeps the
# left hip, the new one went to the right hip, and the reach at the left hip equipped before it drew.)
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/holster-near.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands HolsterNear=1
ini Hands PlaceHintOpacity=0.25
# The reach opens the hand after a draw: WeaponHold (2026-10-24) would drop it.
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
ini Debug EyeDump=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
expect HandScript: finished
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - drawing
count 2 Holster near: the places shown (the weapon hand's weapon, opacity 0.25)
count 2 Holster near: the places hidden
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
wait 20
mark settle
wait 1
mark sword_in_slot
# The left hip: the sword drawn, the hand then up and well away from every place.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 0.10 -0.50
wait 3
mark drawn_away
# Into the right hip's place, the grip open: the hips' circles fade in.
right pos 0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 1
mark near_right_hip
# Up and away: they fade out.
right pos 0.20 0.10 -0.50
wait 1
mark away_again
wait 1
