# Distant water flickering (Nadi, 2026-11-06: far-off water reflections
# flicker, like the foliage did). One of three runs that tell its source
# apart: WaterReflectionMode=2 here (capture) - 2 OBVR's stable capture (live),
# 0 the engine's own reflection, 1 nothing mirrored (waves, depth, sun only).
# Flicker that stays in the 1 run comes from the waves, not the reflection.
# At the rocky north shore of Lake Rumare (as water-turn-jitter.txt), the
# game's clock at its normal pace, the right eye written at every frame:
# thirty frames with the head still, thirty turning a fiftieth of a degree a
# frame (the head's own small motion in the headset). Read with
# tools/water-far-flicker.py: the frame-to-frame change per screen row, far
# water against near.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
ini Render WaterReflectionMode=2
ini Debug EyeDump=1
ini Debug EyeDumpFrames=30
console-at ready cow Tamriel 1 15
console-at placed player.setpos y 66400
console-at placed fw 38EEE

expect HandScript: finished
expect a world frame while the game loads
expect the right eye's frames in a row written up to still-f29
expect the right eye's frames in a row written up to micro-f29
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 20
mark placed
wait 6
head pitch -8
head yaw 120
wait 3
mark still
wait 8
head spin 0.02
mark micro
wait 8
head spin 0
wait 1
mark end
