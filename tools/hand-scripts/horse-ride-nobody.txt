# Full VR on a horse WITHOUT the visible body ([Body] Visible=0): the same
# ride as horse-ride.txt, to tell whether the hover and the sway of the
# fifth run of 2026-10-18 (the eyes 245 units over the feet, swinging 59
# units sideways with the head still) come from the body's placement under
# the headset (PlayerBody.cpp moves the third-person root each frame; the
# engine's mounted camera may be built from that skeleton).
#
# Full VR on a horse (a Quest 3 tester, 2026-10-17: "Every time I get on a
# horse, I end up hovering about two meters above it and start swaying from
# side to side. Furthermore, while riding, if I turn my head to the left, the
# camera turns to the right, and vice versa"). The harness save's player is
# moved to Prior Maborel's paint horse at Weynon Priory (WeynonHorsePlayer,
# 0002008C; player.moveto loads its exterior - "coc WeynonPriory" in the
# first run of 2026-10-18 dropped the player at 2048 2048 into nothing, the
# cell named so has no ground there) and mounts it the way a player does:
# the player is put 100 units south of the paint horse (player.moveto
# 0002008C 0 -100 0 - "0002008C.moveto player -85 85 0" in the sixth to
# eighth runs left the horse where it stood, as "JauffreRef.moveto player"
# left Jauffre in perf-jauffre.txt; "the interface answered 0" for such lines
# is no refusal, player.placeatme answers 0 and places), the head turned 45
# degrees right onto it (the player's heading is -45 after the move) and
# bowed 12 degrees - the engine's activation ray is the head's
# (SetWorldPickDirection), the crosshair has to rest on the horse - and A
# pressed once two seconds later, before the horse wanders ("0002008C.Activate player 0/1" from the console ran and
# mounted nobody - "the interface answered 0" - in those runs). The camera's
# ancestry is logged at the mount ("Camera: the camera node's ancestry ...
# the player has mounted") with the camera's transforms against the player's
# feet; "Camera: at the mark" gives the eyes' place and heading at each mark,
# so the hover is the eyes' height over the feet, the sway the eyes' place
# from one mark to the next with the head still, and the turn's sense the
# heading at yaw-left against yaw-right (the synthetic head turned 45
# degrees each way). Then the horse walked ahead for five seconds on the
# left stick. A is not pressed again - on a horse it dismounts. The vanity
# camera is held off with xOBSE's SetNumericINISetting.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/horse-ride-nobody.txt --run-timeout 300 --direct
