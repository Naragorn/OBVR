# The bow's shot with the head tilted (Nadi, Windows headset 2026-11-14: "bogenschiessen oft fliegt der pfeil
# noch ganz woanders hin als wo man zielt"): the head looks 45 degrees down while the bow, upright, points 30
# degrees to the right of it and level. The aim's source has to get that turn - 30 degrees to the right, level -
# and not the 39 the head's own frame reads (vr::LevelAim, archery_test). The arrow is nocked 14 cm behind the bow
# along its line, drawn 35 cm back and loosed. Read in the run's log: "the shot's line -3x degrees to the left of
# the head's heading" (the bow's own line lies a few degrees off its controller's, bow-by-hand.txt), and
# "Aim: an attack takes the gaze at the source - heading A set to B": B - A the same turn in radians.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands BowByHand=1
# The harness save's question box (Lovers with PK.esm active without its esp, since 2026-10-31) closed first.
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.additem 00025231 1
console player.equipitem 00025231
console-at arrows player.additem 00017829 20
console-at arrows_on player.equipitem 00017829
# The console's equipitem equips the arrows one at a time: each shot leaves
# none equipped, the quiver gone and the next draw refused (2026-10-01:
# re-equipped after a shot, the next one drew; not re-equipped, it did not).
# In a game equipped from the inventory the whole stack is - the tester shot
# one after the other. So they are equipped again after the first loose.
console-at loosed player.equipitem 00017829


expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: bow by hand - an arrow taken from the quiver
expect Hands: bow by hand - the arrow nocked
expect Hands: bow by hand - drawn - the engine's draw begins
expect Hands: bow by hand - the shot's line -
expect Hands: bow by hand - loosed
expect Hands: bow by hand - the arrow left
reject Hands: bow by hand - NO ARROW
reject Hands: bow by hand - eased back
reject the shot's line -39
reject the shot's line -4
reject HandScript: WARNING
