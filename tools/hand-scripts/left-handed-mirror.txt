# Left-handed play with the first-person skeleton drawn mirrored ([Hands]
# LeftHandMirror=1, the default; a tester, 2026-10-17: "left handed mode
# inverts hands to face the wrong direction" - the game's right hand, with
# the right hand's calibration, sat on the left controller). The roles
# swapped (LeftHanded=1), both controllers held before the face, then raised
# and turned with the head. At every mark the line "Hands: at the mark - the
# first-person skeleton mirrored: root determinant -1, ..." must say the
# root and both hand bones are turned over (determinant -1), each hand bone
# sitting the grip's length from its own controller's origin (the same
# length as in the control run left-handed-plain.txt, the mirror off), and
# Camera01 - the engine's camera anchor inside the skeleton - where the
# control run has it, the eyes of "Camera: at the mark" too. "Left-handed
# mirror: a first-person draw culled" says the renderer swapped the cull for
# the mirrored draws.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/left-handed-mirror.txt --run-timeout 240 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands LeftHanded=1
ini Hands LeftHandMirror=1
ini Hands HandsInMenus=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: at the mark - the first-person skeleton mirrored: root determinant -1
expect Left-handed mirror: a first-person draw culled
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the first-person skeleton mirrored: root determinant 1
reject the weapon hand's bone determinant 1

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.30 -0.40
left pos -0.25 -0.30 -0.40
wait 2
mark rest
right pos 0.15 -0.10 -0.35
left pos -0.15 -0.10 -0.35
right rot -30 0 0
left rot -30 0 0
wait 2
mark raised
head yaw 40
wait 2
mark turned
head yaw 0
wait 1
mark end
