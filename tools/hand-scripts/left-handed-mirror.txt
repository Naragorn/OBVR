# Left-handed play: the first-person skeleton drawn mirrored (a tester,
# 2026-10-17: "left handed mode inverts hands to face the wrong direction" -
# the game's right hand, with the right hand's calibration, sat on the left
# controller). The roles swapped (LeftHanded=1), both controllers held
# before the face, then raised and turned with the head. At every mark the
# line "Hands: at the mark - the first-person skeleton mirrored: root
# determinant -1, ..." must say the root and both hand bones are turned over
# (determinant -1), each hand bone sitting the grip's length from its own
# controller's origin (13.7 and 11.8 units on 2026-10-20, the same as
# unmirrored), and Camera01 near the eyes; the eyes of "Camera: at the mark"
# must stand where a right-hander's run has them. "Left-handed mirror: a
# first-person draw culled" says the renderer swapped the cull for the
# mirrored draws. The body's arms reach the hands across (the right arm the
# first-person left hand bone, on the right controller) and the first-person
# arms are laid onto them through the skins' bind-pose partners: "Body IK:
# the arm bones' partners for the mirror read from the body's skins", both
# "Body IK: the ... arm follows its hand" lines, and at the marks "the ... arm
# reaches its hand (the wrist N units off it ...) ... the first-person arm on
# it within N units, M degrees" - small numbers mean the cuff meets the
# sleeve.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/left-handed-mirror.txt --run-timeout 240 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands LeftHanded=1
ini Hands HandsInMenus=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: at the mark - the first-person skeleton mirrored: root determinant -1
expect Left-handed mirror: a first-person draw culled
expect Body IK: the arm bones' partners for the mirror read from the body's skins
expect Body IK: the right arm follows its hand
expect Body IK: the left arm follows its hand
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the first-person skeleton mirrored: root determinant 1
reject the weapon hand's bone determinant 1
reject Body IK: left-handed play without the mirror
reject the mirror's bone partners did not read

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.30 -0.40
left pos -0.25 -0.30 -0.40
wait 2
mark rest
right pos 0.15 -0.10 -0.35
left pos -0.15 -0.10 -0.35
right rot -30 0 0
left rot -30 0 0
wait 2
mark raised
head yaw 40
wait 2
mark turned
head yaw 0
wait 1
mark end
