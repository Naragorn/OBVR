# The sheathed weapon on a left-hander's side (Nadi in the headset,
# 2026-10-21: "waffe ziehen mit links ja das geht. aber die waffe sheathed
# ist weiterhin an linker hüfte obwohl man die an rechter hüfte zieht").
# The game hangs the sheathed one-hander on "SideWeapon" under Bip01 Pelvis
# at the left hip and the quiver on "Quiver" under Spine2 at the right
# (skeleton.nif); with LeftHanded=1 the body IK mirrors them across the
# body's middle in their parents' frames (vr/SheathMirror.h,
# MirroredAcrossLocalZ) - the one-hander to the right hip, where the left
# hand draws it, the quiver to the left. The harness save carries a
# sheathed one-hander (the tree shows "Weapon  (000229B3)" under
# SideWeapon). What it proves: the mirror line for both nodes with the
# sides as the engine has them this frame, the tree's world places, and a
# picture looking down at the hips. The control is sheath-right-handed.txt.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/sheath-left-handed.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands LeftHanded=1
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
ini Debug BodyTreeProbe=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Body: the sheath "SideWeapon" mirrored for the left hand - under "Bip01 Pelvis"
expect to the body's left, now
expect Body: the sheath "Quiver" mirrored for the left hand - under "Bip01 Spine2"
reject left where the game hangs it
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 10
right pos 0.25 -0.30 -0.30
left pos -0.25 -0.30 -0.30
head pitch -70
wait 3
mark look-down
head yaw -35
wait 3

head yaw 35
