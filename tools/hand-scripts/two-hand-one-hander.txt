# Holding a one-hander with both hands (vr/TwoHandLogic.h, TwoHandOneHanders):
# an Iron Longsword drawn from the left hip, the left grip closed below the
# right hand - held with both hands, the game's two-handed grip taken from its
# files (a one-hander's own animation keeps the left hand at the side), the
# hand cupping the short handle's pommel end; the grip opened - let go. While
# held, the left grip grabs nothing.
# TwoHandReachUnits is widened for the run: where the scripted hands put the
# handle is not measured.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands TwoHandGrip=1
ini Hands TwoHandOneHanders=1
ini Hands TwoHandReachUnits=60
console player.additem 000229B3 1
console player.equipitem 000229B3

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: the open left hand near the one-hander's handle takes its shape
expect Hands: the left hand took the one-hander's handle
expect Hands: the left hand drawn on the handle
expect Hands: the left hand's fingers close round the handle
expect Hands: the left hand let go of the handle (the grip opened)
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Hands: grab - left grip

wait 20
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn
left pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.10
wait 0.5
left grip 1
wait 1
mark both_hands
left pos 0.18 -0.42 -0.14
wait 1
mark slid
left grip 0
wait 1
mark let_go
