# The kick's foot goes where the eyes look (Nadi, 2026-11-01: "der kick fuss
# sollte whl höher gehen je nachdem wo man hinschaut"; game::KickPeak,
# KickLookShare 1.5): the right B held with the head level, then with it
# pitched 20 degrees up - the foot at the mark is higher the second time
# (level: about 48 units above the feet; up: the drop from 20 to -10 degrees,
# the foot above the hip, some 70). Since 2026-11-03 (KickDropDegrees -30,
# the look raises only): level the foot 30 degrees above the hip, up 50 (the
# cap), and a third kick looking 30 degrees down leaves it as level - "the
# foot at its peak ... under the eyes' level" says where it sat in the view.
# Since the look-down aim (KickPeakOnLook): the third and a fourth kick
# looking 55 down put the foot on the look's line - "under the look" near 0.
# Run with: powershell -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\kick-look-up.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Kick=1
ini Hands KickLookShare=1.5
console-at settle CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
expect Kick: 1 - the right leg out
expect Kick: 2 - the right leg out
expect Kick: 3 - the right leg out
expect Kick: 4 - the right leg out
expect Kick: 1 - the foot at its peak
expect Kick: at the mark the foot is
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
mark settle
wait 2
right b 1
wait 0.5
mark kick_level
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch 20
wait 0.8
right b 1
wait 0.5
mark kick_up
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch -30
wait 0.8
right b 1
wait 0.5
mark kick_down
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch -55
wait 0.8
right b 1
wait 0.5
mark kick_steep
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch 0
wait 0.5
