# The spell ring on the other hand (docs/controls-spec.md 4.44; the tester,
# 2026-10-15: "linker stick press nicht mehr quests sondern eine zauberbuch
# als hotkey wie wir das schon rechts haben"): a short click of the left
# stick opens the player's spellbook on the ring, latched; the hand moved up
# lights slot 1, the next click makes that spell the active one. Then the
# left stick held past StickHoldSeconds: the run, and no ring on its release.
# Flare (000A97DF, "All players start with this spell", UESP) is added by the
# console so the book is never empty whatever the save's character knows;
# the harness character has it already, with Heal Minor Wounds and the
# Lover's Kiss power, so the ring shows the three in the order of the names.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/spell-ring.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands SpellRing=1
console player.addspell 000A97DF

expect HandScript: finished
count 1 SpellRing: opened at the other hand
expect the spell ring's picture saved as OBVR-SpellRing-ring_open.bmp
expect the spell ring's picture saved as OBVR-SpellRing-slot1_lit.bmp
expect 3 spell(s) known, page 1 of 1: 1 Flare, 2 Heal Minor Wounds, 3 Lover's Kiss, 4 -
expect SpellRing: slot 1 used (Flare
expect the engine took it; the active spell now Flare
reject SpellRing: closed without a spell
reject SpellRing: the player's base form could not be read
reject QuickMenu: opened at the hand
reject Hands: menu SleepWait
reject HandScript: WARNING

# Time for the runner to type the console lines and the console to close.
wait 16
mark standing
left pos -0.15 -0.30 -0.35
wait 0.3
# A short click: the ring, latched.
left click 1
wait 0.12
left click 0
wait 0.6
mark ring_open
# Up from where it opened: slot 1.
left pos -0.15 -0.22 -0.35
wait 0.4
mark slot1_lit
# The next click lets go: slot 1's spell made the active one.
left click 1
wait 0.12
left click 0
wait 1.5
mark after_use
# Held: the run, and its release opens no ring.
left pos -0.15 -0.30 -0.35
wait 0.3
left click 1
wait 0.9
left click 0
wait 1.0
mark after_hold
wait 1
