# Eighteen Walkers fighting the player, the twin of the Walkers session's
# perf-walkers-in-view.txt (2026-10-26, ImperialDungeon01, eighteen Walkers
# placed at the player, their AI switched off after eight seconds - standing
# people cost +3.3 ms a pass there). Here the AI stays on: the Walkers attack
# an untouchable (tgm), restrained player with collision off, as Nadi's
# Windows run of 2026-10-26 had it in Fort Urasek before its crash (18
# Walkers in sight, 22 to 23 ms frames at 90 Hz). The marks and times are
# the twin's, so perf-phases.py and perf-functions.py compare phase by
# phase: "prison" to "empty-watched" is the empty prison, "people-placed" to
# "people-watched" the fight. What differs between the two runs is the cost
# of fighting over standing - the combat AI, the hit reactions, the ragdolls.
# The sampler runs for the profile by function (tools/perf-functions.py,
# OBVR.dll's own hooks with the scratch obvr_symbols.py and OBVR.map).
#
# Timing: the capture (95 s) is armed 60 s after the game starts, about 40 s
# into the script, and closes around "people-watched"; the 45 s after it are
# for the export.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/perf-walkers-fight.txt --headless --save OBVRHarness --run-timeout 420
