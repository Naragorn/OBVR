# The left hand's fingers after a charged spell (Nadi's Windows run of 2026-11-26: "konnte ich die
# linke hand nicht mehr zumachen, finger tracking ging auch nicht mehr"). The charged spell's orb
# (game/SpellOrb.h) is a copy of the spell's model hung on Bip01 L Hand and kept there, hidden by
# the cull bit, between charges. game::HandHoldsItem took that hidden copy for a held item, so
# from the first charge on the left fingers stayed as the animation had them: no fist, no tracking.
# Here: Flare charged 2.5 s and let go, then the left hand curled into a fist and opened again.
# The left hand must hold nothing once the orb is gone, and its fingers follow the controller.
# Since 2026-11-26 (the tester said yes) they follow it while the spell charges too: the orb, shown
# or hidden, is never a held item (game::HandChildHoldsItem) - a fist is made at the charged mark.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands SpellCharge=1
ini Hands FingerTracking=1
# The fist gesture off: the curled hand here is only a pose.
ini Hands Fists=0
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.addspell 000A97DF
console SetActiveSpell 000A97DF

expect HandScript: finished
expect Spell orb: "SpecialIdle_Projectile" playing
expect Spell orb: gone from the hand
# The orb is no held item, shown or hidden: the left hand holds nothing of its own, said once.
count 1 Hands: the left hand holds nothing of its own
reject Fireball.NIF" - its fingers stay as the animation has them
expect Hands: at the mark charged-fist - the left fingers follow the controller
expect Hands: at the mark fist-after-cast - the left fingers follow the controller
expect Hands: at the mark open-after-cast - the left fingers follow the controller
reject HandScript: WARNING

left index 1
wait 12
mark settle
wait 1
right pos 0.25 -0.60 -0.10
left pos 0.02 -0.15 -0.35
left rot 0 0 -90
left curl 0
wait 1.5
mark before-cast
left trigger 1
wait 2.5
mark charged
left curl 1
wait 1
mark charged-fist
left curl 0
wait 0.5
left trigger 0
wait 3
mark after-cast
left curl 1
wait 1.5
mark fist-after-cast
left curl 0
wait 1.5
mark open-after-cast
wait 1
