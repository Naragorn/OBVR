# A weapon pulled to the hand by one short grip under the grip toggle (Nadi, Windows 2026-11-27, GripToggle=1:
# "some of them shot out past my hand, so they were not equipped but shot on"; free-weapons-spec, "Windows run of
# 2026-11-27"). The sword dropped at the feet, the weapon hand about a metre above it, the grip closed for 0.1 s and
# opened while the sword is still on its way: with the toggle the hand keeps what the pull brings (game/PullHold.h),
# so the grab stays on, the sword arrives, is taken, equipped and drawn, and the hand keeps it.
# Before the fix: the grip's opening let the grab go mid-flight - "grab - grip open, the object let go", "let go of
# ... set down (0 units/s)", then "flight of ... after the release" at 500-700 units/s and no take.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-pull-toggle.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands GrabEquipsWeapons=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands PullToHand=1
ini Hands PullReachMetres=2.0
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands GripToggle=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.drop 00000C0C 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect a grip pulls it to the hand
expect Grip toggle: the weapon hand took hold - kept closed until the next grip
expect pulled to the hand and taken
expect Weapon grab: equipped and drawn - straight into the hand
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject NOT taken
reject stayed in the engine's grab
reject the pull given up
reject Grip toggle: the weapon hand lost what it kept
reject Weapon hold: let go

wait 15
head pitch -50
right pos 0.05 -0.55 -0.70
right rot -80 0 0
wait 1.5
mark near
right grip 0.9
wait 0.1
right grip 0
mark opened
wait 3
mark drawn
wait 2
mark kept
