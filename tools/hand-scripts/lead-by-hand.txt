# Taking someone by the hand (game/LeadLogic.h, game/Lead.h): a beggar is
# placed near, the right grip closes and takes their hand, they are given
# Oblivion.esm's FollowPlayer package and walk after the player while the
# player walks ahead (the left stick), liking the player less per metre;
# letting go takes the package away again. LeadTakeUnits and LeadBreakUnits
# are widened for the run: where the beggar's hand ends up is not known to
# the script, and it follows at a walk.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands LeadByHand=1
ini Hands LeadTakeUnits=200
ini Hands LeadBreakUnits=2000
ini Hands PushPeople=0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Lead: the right hand took
expect given FollowPlayer
expect m, their hand
expect let go - FollowPlayer taken away
reject Lead: the package functions are not the bytes read
reject HandScript: WARNING

console player.placeatme 0001C45B 1
wait 10
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark before
right grip 1
wait 1
mark held
left stick 0 1
wait 4
left stick 0 0
wait 1
mark walked
right grip 0
wait 1
mark released
wait 1
