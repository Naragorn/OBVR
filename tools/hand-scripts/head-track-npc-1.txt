# The engine's head tracking on the player (game/PlayerHeadTrack.h): a beggar
# is placed 120 units to the player's right (PlaceAtMe direction 3) - the
# engine's head tracking measures from the actor's heading, which the
# headset does not turn - then the head turned 70 degrees each way, so the
# body faces away from them - the engine's head tracking, picking a nearby
# actor every 0.2-0.4 s, would turn the head back to them and twist the spine.
# PlayerHeadTracking=1 here (the control: the engine's own head tracking).
# "Body: head tracking at the mark" gives the object's state, "Body IK: at the
# mark" the chest's twist OBVR took out.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\head-track-npc-1.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Body Visible=1
ini Body IK=1
ini Body PlayerHeadTracking=1
console-at ready player.placeatme 0001C45B 1 120 3

expect HandScript: finished
expect Body: head tracking at the mark
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.30 -0.20 -0.45
left pos -0.30 -0.20 -0.45
mark ready
wait 3
mark facing
head yaw 70
wait 4
mark turned-away
wait 3
mark turned-away-later
head yaw -70
wait 4
mark turned-other-way
