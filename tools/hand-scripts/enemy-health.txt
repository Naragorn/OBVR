# The enemy health bars over the fighters ([HandHud] EnemyHealthBars,
# game/EnemyHealthLogic.h, game/EnemyHealth.h): two melee bandits (00069AD1)
# attack an untouchable player (tgm) who stands still; each gets a bar of
# its own above its head ("Enemy health: a bar over ... above the head
# bone"), the state at every mark says how full, how far and whether the
# overlay is up; at fight3 the console kills them all (killall), so the bars
# go empty, linger two seconds and go ("bar went"). The game's own bar,
# when the engine has it up, is logged as hidden ("the game's own bar").
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\enemy-health.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini HandHud EnemyHealthBars=1
ini HandHud EnemyHealthMetres=25
ini Hands CombatReach=85
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 2
console-at ready tgm
console-at fight3 killall

expect HandScript: finished
expect Enemy health: a bar over
expect above the head bone
expect Enemy health: at the mark - bar 1 over
expect Enemy health: at the mark - bar 2 over
expect the overlay up
expect bar went
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the overlay NOT up

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 10
mark fight1
wait 4
mark fight2
wait 4
mark fight3
wait 8
mark dead1
wait 3
mark dead2
wait 1
