# game/EnemyHealthLogic.h, game/EnemyHealth.h, game/EnemyHealthModel.h): two
# melee bandits (00069AD1) attack an untouchable player (tgm) who stands
# still; each gets a bar of its own above its head ("Enemy health: a bar
# over ... above the head bone") - the game's own bar's frames, found under
# HUDReticle's enemy health tile ("the game's own bar model found") and read
# off the game's textures ("read off the game's texture"), on OBVR's
# overlays. Since 2026-10-25 a bar shows only while its fighter is hurt, in
# sight of the eyes, for 4 s after the health last moved: at fight1 both
# are at full health and their bars are kept hidden ("hidden - not hurt");
# the console then takes 30 health off the nearest ({actor}), whose bar
# comes up ("bar shown", at hurt1 "the overlay up (the game's own frame")
# and fades 4 s later ("faded - "); at fight2 the nearest is hurt again,
# and at fight3 the console kills them all (killall). Since 2026-10-26 a

# overlay up (the game's own frame 40 of 40)" at dead0), the one at full

# fight2 being the one hurt at fight1, as in every run so far. OBVR's own painted bar is the fallback and
# must not show. The synthetic head faces the nearest fighter (head face
# 1), pitched up 25 degrees so the bar above a close bandit's head is in the
# frame, and the eyes are dumped at the marks (dump-*-eyes.png) - the
# monitor's picture is the game's own camera, not the headset's.
