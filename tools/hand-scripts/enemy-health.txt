# The enemy health bars over the fighters ([HandHud] EnemyHealthBars,
# game/EnemyHealthLogic.h, game/EnemyHealth.h, game/EnemyHealthModel.h): two
# melee bandits (00069AD1) attack an untouchable player (tgm) who stands
# still; each gets a bar of its own above its head ("Enemy health: a bar
# over ... above the head bone") - the game's own bar's frames, found under
# HUDReticle's enemy health tile ("the game's own bar model found") and read
# off the game's textures ("read off the game's texture"), on OBVR's
# overlays; the state at every mark says how full, how far and which frame
# is up; at fight3 the console kills them all (killall), so the bars go
# empty, linger two seconds and go ("bar went"). OBVR's own painted bar is
# the fallback and must not show. The synthetic head faces the
# nearest fighter (head face 1), pitched up 25 degrees so the bar above a
# close bandit's head is in the frame, and the eyes are dumped at the fight
# marks (dump-*-eyes.png), so the arc over the head can be seen - the
# monitor's picture is the game's own camera, not the headset's.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\enemy-health.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini HandHud EnemyHealthBars=1
ini HandHud EnemyHealthMetres=25
ini Hands CombatReach=85
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 2
console-at ready tgm
console-at fight3 killall

expect HandScript: finished
expect Enemy health: the game's own bar model found
expect Enemy health: the bars are the game's own frames
expect read off the game's texture
expect Enemy health: a bar over
expect above the head bone
expect Enemy health: at the mark - bar 1 over
expect Enemy health: at the mark - bar 2 over
expect the overlay up (the game's own frame
expect bar went
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the overlay NOT up
reject could not be read off the game's texture
reject OBVR's own bar
reject the bars are OBVR's own

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 1
head pitch 25
head face 1
wait 9
mark fight1
dump
wait 4
mark fight2
dump
wait 4
mark fight3
wait 8
mark dead1
wait 3
mark dead2
wait 1
