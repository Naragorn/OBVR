# A torch equipped into an open hand has to be held ([Hands] TorchGraceSeconds;
# game/TorchRelease.h; Nadi, 2026-10-27: "gibt zeit um die hand zu schliessen
# damit sie nicht sofort zu boden fällt. aber ich muss sie schon halten,
# sonst fällt sie"). Torch02 (0002CF9F, two of them) equipped by the console
# with the other hand open: it falls after the two seconds, never held. The
# second equipped at a mark, the hand closing within the second: armed, held
# for five seconds, still in the hand; then let go - it falls as any.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/torch-grace.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands TorchDrop=1
ini Hands TorchGraceSeconds=2
console player.additem 0002CF9F 2
console-at ready player.equipitem 0002CF9F
console-at second player.equipitem 0002CF9F

expect HandScript: finished
expect never held (TorchGraceSeconds)
expect Torch: the hold closed on the torch - letting go drops it, burning
count 2 Torch: let go - the new reference
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left grip 0
mark ready
wait 5
mark fell
wait 2
mark second
wait 1
left grip 0.9
wait 5
mark held
left grip 0
wait 2
mark let_go
wait 1
