# The fighters' ring ([Hands] CombatRing, game/CombatRing.h, combat-comfort-
# spec 4): three melee bandits (00069AD1, attacks on sight) placed by an
# untouchable player (tgm). One has the turn and comes in; the other two wait
# at the ring - kept 3 m off here (CombatRingMetres=3, a room's size) - and
# walk round the player. The state lines at each mark give each one's
# distance, turn or waiting, and order (0 free, 1 back, 2 round); after the
# first blows the turn passes ("turn(s) ended").
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\combat-ring.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands CombatRing=1
ini Hands CombatRingMetres=3
ini Hands MeleeAttackersAtOnce=1
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 3
console-at ready tgm

expect HandScript: finished
expect Combat ring: 3 fighting the player - 1 with a turn, 2 waiting
expect turn(s) ended
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 10
mark fight1
wait 3
mark fight2
wait 3
mark fight3
wait 3
mark fight4
wait 3
mark fight5
wait 3
mark fight6
