# Enemy attacks at half speed ([Hands] EnemyAttackSpeed=50, game/AttackTempo.h,
# combat-comfort-spec 4 item 1): one melee bandit (00069AD1) at an
# untouchable player (tgm), the ring off so it attacks as it will. Each of
# its attack sequences is followed while it plays; when it ends the log says
# how much of its animation ran in how much of the clock, against the
# sequence's own rate (the weapon's speed, 0.8-1.2 for the bandit's weapons,
# read over the first frame followed) - "x0.50 read back" - and the state
# lines at the marks list what is followed and how fast the swings seen ran
# in all. At the game's own speed (attack-tempo-vanilla.txt) the swings'
# time runs at the weapon's speed; here at half of it.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\attack-tempo.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands CombatRing=0
ini Hands EnemyAttackSpeed=50
ini Hands EnemyAttackFrequency=100
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 1
console-at ready tgm

expect HandScript: finished
expect Attack tempo: the anim update's key firing - the call at 00477AB1
expect followed from
expect x0.50 asked, x0.50 read back
expect attack action lasted
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 10
mark fight1
wait 5
mark fight2
wait 5
mark fight3
wait 5
