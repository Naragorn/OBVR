# Doors by grip (vr/DoorGrip.h): an interior door pushed open. A plain door
# (Oblivion.esm's doorfulllower, 0002318B, read off the plugin 2026-11-11) is
# placed 60 units ahead; the empty right hand comes to it at handle height,
# its grip closes, the hand goes 18 cm on and the door is activated as A
# activates it ("opened by the grip, as A"). Which way a door swings is not
# known to OBVR: a push opens it as a pull does. PlaceAtMe sets the door's
# origin - the middle of its model - at the player's feet, so the placed door
# stands half in the floor (measured 2026-11-11: its box -1.46..0.01 across,
# -1.04..1.04 m high about its origin, the leaf left of its origin and 0.8 m
# ahead); its handle band is therefore at the floor, and the hand goes there.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/door-grip-push.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands DoorsByGrip=1
ini Hands DoorGripMoveMetres=0.12
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready player.placeatme 0002318B 1 60 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Door: state - the nearest door
expect taken at the handle by the right hand
expect opened by the grip, as A
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 10
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
mark ready
wait 4
mark placed
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.50
wait 0.5
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.66
wait 0.5
mark at
right grip 1
wait 0.3
mark before
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.72
wait 0.2
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.78
wait 0.2
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.84
wait 0.2
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.86
wait 1
mark after
right grip 0
wait 2
mark end
wait 1
