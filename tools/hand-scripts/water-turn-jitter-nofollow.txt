# The control of the control: RoomScaleBody=0, the body never stepped after the head - if the water's
# jumps every four to five frames (runs of 2026-11-02) go with the room's steps, they are gone here.
# The control for water-turn-jitter.txt: the same place, weather, head and
# frames with the reflection map at the engine's own 256 texels a side
# ([Render] WaterReflectionMapSize=0). Its turning run's excess over its
# still run is the texel jump as the game ships it; water-turn-jitter.txt
# at 1024 must show a smaller one.
# Run with: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\water-turn-jitter-nofollow.txt -Direct
# Then:     powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\water-turn-jitter.ps1 -Run <the run's folder>

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands RoomScaleBody=0
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
ini Render WaterReflectionMode=2
ini Render WaterReflectionMapSize=0
ini Debug EyeDump=1
ini Debug EyeDumpFrames=30
ini Debug WaterCaptureTrace=1
console-at ready cow Tamriel 1 15
console-at placed player.setpos y 66400
console-at placed fw 38EEE
console-at placed sgtm 0.01
console-at end sgtm 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect a world frame while the game loads
expect Water reflection map: 256 x 256 texels, the engine's own
expect Water reflection target: 256 x 256 texels
expect Water capture: the eyes look out to tangents
expect the right eye's frames in a row written up to still-f29
expect the right eye's frames in a row written up to turn-f29
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 20
mark placed
wait 6
head pitch -15
head yaw 120
wait 3
mark still
wait 8
head spin 0.1
mark turn
wait 8
head spin 0
wait 1
mark end
