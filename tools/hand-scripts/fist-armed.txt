# Fists with a weapon in the slot (docs/controls-spec.md 4.3): the sword
# drawn, a fist is the hand round it - nothing happens; the sword sheathed,
# a fist takes it off and raises the fists.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\fist-armed.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Fists=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Fist: the weapon hand closed - nothing to ready
expect Fist: the weapon hand closed - the sheathed weapon taken off for the fists
expect Fist: the slot is empty - fists up
reject could NOT be taken off
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark sword_sheathed

# Draw the sword from the hip, then make a fist round it: nothing.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 2.5
mark sword_drawn
right curl 1
wait 1
mark fist_on_sword
right curl 0
wait 1

# Sheathe it by the same reach, then a fist: the sword comes off, fists up.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 2.5
mark sword_sheathed_again
right curl 1
wait 2.5
mark fists_up
wait 1
