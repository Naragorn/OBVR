# The spell's line from the casting hand with the head tilted (vr/SpellAim.h AimAlong; controls-spec, "the
# weapon hand's aim and with it the spell's line have the same error with a tilted head"). Flare from the raised
# left hand turned 30 degrees to the right, cast twice with the hand where it is: once with the head level, once
# with the head looking 45 degrees down. The aim's turn is the laser's heading less the head's, both flat, so
# both casts read the same turn in their "Spell: ... the aim along the laser (turn X" lines. Read in the head's
# own frame, as before the fix, the second cast's turn came out larger (spell_aim_test: 30 degrees read as 39).

ini Hands Enabled=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.addspell 000A97DF
console SetActiveSpell 000A97DF

expect HandScript: finished
expect the aim along the laser
expect Aim: the spell starts at the casting hand
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark settle
wait 1
right pos 0.25 -0.60 -0.10
left pos -0.15 -0.30 -0.45
left rot 0 -30 0
wait 1.5
mark level
dump
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 0.5
mark cast-level
dump
wait 2.5
head pitch -45
wait 1
mark looking-down
dump
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 0.5
mark cast-looking-down
dump
wait 2.5
