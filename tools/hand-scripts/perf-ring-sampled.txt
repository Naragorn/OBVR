# The performance ring with the render sampler on ([Performance] Sampler=1,
# render/RenderSampler.h): the sampler's rows are a ring too and follow every
# save into its folder - the save in the middle (action save_last_minute)
# and the one at the quit - cut to the ring's RingSeconds like the events.
# Before 2026-11-14 the sampler kept the first 300 000 rows of a recording
# and a ring could begin again before the rows had the save's folder (they
# then went beside the log). perf-ring.txt is the ring without the sampler.
# Read after: samples.csv in both OBVR-performance-N folders - its ticks lie
# inside manifest.json's start_tick..end_tick, at most RingSeconds long.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/perf-ring-sampled.txt --headless --run-timeout 240
