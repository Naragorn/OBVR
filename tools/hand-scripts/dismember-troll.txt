# creatures losing their heads; docs/dismemberment-spec.md "2026-11-26: beasts").

# Troll01:0 holds it, 1064 of its 5476 vertices mostly on the head bone or under
# it (pyffi, tools/nif-head-share.py). The shape is the body's by its bones (13 of

# bare (the probe of 2026-11-26, 20261126-165112-slot2: "Troll01:0(body, 13/61)",

reject - its cut forgotten
