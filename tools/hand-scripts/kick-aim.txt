# The kick aimed by the look (game::KickLookTarget, KickPeakAt; 2026-11-03):
# a beggar (0001C45B) placed 50 units ahead (70 is past the leg's reach -
# the first run met no one there) and held still (tai), the player
# untouchable (tgm). Three kicks, the head pitched 0, -20 and -45 degrees:
# each kick logs whom and which part the look met at its start ("Kick: N
# aimed at ...'s <part>") and the foot goes there - the head or neck, the
# torso, a leg - and meets him ("Kick: N met"). Last a kick looking away
# (yaw 90): "aimed at no one", the kick by the look as before.
# PushPeople 0, so the stagger does not move him between the kicks.
# Run with: powershell -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\kick-aim.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands VanillaKnockdown=0
ini Hands Kick=1
ini Hands KickAimUnits=110
ini Hands PushPeople=0
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setangle z 0
console-at placed player.placeatme 0001C45B 1 50 0
console-at frozen tai

expect HandScript: finished
expect Kick: 1 aimed at
expect Kick: 2 aimed at
expect Kick: 3 aimed at
expect Kick: 4 aimed at no one
expect Kick: 1 met
expect Kick: 2 met
expect Kick: 3 met
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 1
mark placed
wait 1
mark frozen
wait 1.5
head pitch 0
wait 0.5
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark kick_level
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch -20
wait 0.5
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark kick_chest
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch -45
wait 0.5
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark kick_low
right b 0
wait 1.5
head pitch 0
head yaw 90
wait 0.5
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark kick_away
right b 0
wait 1.5
head yaw 0
wait 0.5
mark end
