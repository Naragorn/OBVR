# An old save made in third person loads in third person (Nadi in the
# headset, 2026-10-22: "teilweise ich alte spielstände lade und ich nicht in
# 1st person lande sondern 3rd"): the harness save loaded, the view switched
# to third person by the game's own POV key (R, keybd_event), a save made
# there from the console, that save loaded from the console - and Full VR
# must put the view into first person once after the load ([Hands]
# FirstPersonAfterLoad, camera/FrameLogic.h StepFirstPersonAfterLoad). The
# mark line "Hands: at the mark - ... third person N" reads the view at each
# mark: 1 at "third" and "saved", 0 at "reloaded".
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/load-third-person.txt --run-timeout 240 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect third person 1
expect Hands: the save was made in third person - put into first person once after the load
expect third person 0
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 15
mark loaded
key 0x52 1
wait 0.2
key 0x52 0
wait 3
mark third
console-at third save OBVRThird
wait 6
mark saved
console-at saved load OBVRThird
wait 25
mark reloaded
wait 3
mark reloaded-later
wait 1
