# The right-handed control for left-handed-mirror.txt: the same poses and
# marks with LeftHanded=0. By symmetry the body's arms must come out the
# same - the right arm reaching the right controller with the same shoulder,
# elbow and hand in "Body IK: at the mark - the right arm reaches its hand",
# the same hand roll on the forearm - whether the right controller carries
# the game's right hand (here) or, mirrored, the game's left hand laid onto
# the body's right arm through the skins' partners (the mirror run). A
# difference in the rolls would be the bone convention wrong.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/left-handed-control.txt --run-timeout 240 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands LeftHanded=0
ini Hands HandsInMenus=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: at the mark - the first-person skeleton as the game has it: root determinant 1
expect Body IK: the right arm follows its hand
expect Body IK: the left arm follows its hand
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Left-handed mirror: a first-person draw culled

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.30 -0.40
left pos -0.25 -0.30 -0.40
wait 2
mark rest
right pos 0.15 -0.10 -0.35
left pos -0.15 -0.10 -0.35
right rot -30 0 0
left rot -30 0 0
wait 2
mark raised
head yaw 40
wait 2
mark turned
head yaw 0
wait 1
mark end
