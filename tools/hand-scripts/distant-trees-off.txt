# The distant land's trees faced by their place (render/DistantTreeFacing.h;
# Nadi in the headset, 2026-10-22: "die ganz weit entfernten bäume bewegen
# sich mit dem headset"), [Render] StableDistantTrees=0 here - distant-trees-on.txt
# and -off.txt are the pair. At the lake under the bridge, clear weather, the
# head at yaw 44 across the wooded hills, then turned 30 degrees: the trees
# have to be drawn as before (none missing, no streaks), and with the switch
# on the patched shader has to be made and set.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/distant-trees-off.txt --run-timeout 240 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
ini Render StableDistantTrees=0
console-at ready cow Tamriel 1 15
console-at placed player.setpos y 66400
console-at placed fw 38EEE

expect HandScript: finished
reject Distant trees: the place-faced shader set
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 20
mark placed
wait 6
head pitch -5
head yaw 44
wait 2
mark ahead
wait 3
head yaw 74
wait 2
mark turned
wait 3
