# The drawing hand at the face (a beta tester, 2026-10-23: "A minor bug with
# drawing the bowstring - when this happens, part of the arm disappears", a
# video of the right hand cut open in front of the eyes with no forearm). The
# bow by hand as in bow-by-hand.txt, with the visible body and its arms' IK
# on: the arrow taken, nocked, drawn, then the hand to the cheek. The bow's
# line runs through the eyes, and on the string the fist is moved onto it
# (vr/Archery.h): before the fix it was moved from the cheek up between the
# eyes there (the "Body IK: at the mark" line's hand at the eyes' height,
# 1.1 units behind them; the eye dumps show the hand cut open by the near
# plane). Now near the eyes it keeps the controller's offset from the line
# (vr::FistTargetOffFace) - the mark "cheek" must find it below the eyes.
