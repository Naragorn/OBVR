# The kick's hit (game/KickLogic.h, physical-combat-spec 11: the cleave, the
# stagger standing, the knockdown running, told as a hit): a bandit
# (00069AD1) placed 55 units ahead, the right B standing - "Kick: 1 met",
# "staggered, told as a hit" - then another 110 units ahead, the left stick
# pressed in and pushed ahead and the right B again: "knocked down (running)".
# The bandits fight back, so where they stand by the second kick varies; two
# met at once (the cleave) is kick_test's, not this run's.
# God mode, so the bandits' blows do not end the run.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/kick-hit.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Kick=1
ini Hands PushPeople=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setangle z 0
console-at placed player.placeatme 00069AD1 1 55 0
console-at after player.placeatme 00069AD1 1 110 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Kick: 1 - the right leg out
expect Kick: 1 met
expect staggered, told as a hit
expect Kick: 2 - the right leg out (running)
expect knocked down (running), told as a hit
reject Kick: 1 met 00000000
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 0.2
right b 1
