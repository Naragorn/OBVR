# Single-pass stereo, A1 (docs/single-pass-stereo-spec.md): people in the
# right eye. The fixed Walkers scene (save OBVRHarnessWalkers) with
# SinglePassStereo=0 and OBVR's eye dumps at each mark; on against off shows
# whether eye 1's skinned bodies stand where the dual pass puts them (the
# bone palettes are camera-relative, render/BoneRebase.h).
#   bash ../OBNG/tools/shadow-run.sh --ini tools/hand-scripts/single-pass-obng.ini tools/hand-scripts/single-pass-people-off.txt --save OBVRHarnessWalkers

ini Render SinglePassStereo=0
ini Debug EyeDump=1
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready tcl
console-at ready player.setrestrained 1
console-at ready SetNumericINISetting "fVanityModeAutoDelay:Camera" 100000
console-at ready tai

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 1
mark ready
wait 20
mark people
wait 2
mark people_again
