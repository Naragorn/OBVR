# Standing, the head turned and tilted to look about - the body must not
# walk after it, and must not turn after a short look (Nadi in the headset,
# 2026-11-10: turning or tilting the head to look left or right should not
# move the body at once). The scripted head swings the eyes about the neck
# as a real one does (test/HandScriptPose.h), 4.6 cm for a 35-degree look,
# past RoomScaleStart's 2 units: before 2026-11-10 the room follow walked the
# player after the eyes (vr/RoomFollowLogic.h, NeckPivotOffset) and the torso
# closed on the head within a second (game/BodyTorsoLogic.h).
#
# Read with: tools/body-motion.py <run> --gate look:root<=1.5 --gate glance:root<=1.5
#   --gate look:steps<=0 --gate glance:steps<=0 --gate tilt:steps<=0 --gate held:said>=1
#   (root: the skeleton under the eyes, which swing with the look; the player
#   itself must not walk - the "still" lines below)
# The weather pinned (fw) so frame times are alike from run to run.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/body-look-standing.txt --headless

console-at ready CloseAllMenus
