# Half-swording (vr/HalfSwordLogic.h): an Iron Claymore drawn, the left hand
# brought open to its blade 0.4 m ahead of the right hand - it takes the
# grip's shape on the way in - and the grip closed there: the left hand holds
# the blade with the game's own grip, above the right hand. The trigger held,
# it slides up the blade; the grip opened, it lets go.
# TwoHandReachUnits is widened for the run, as in two-hand-grip.txt: where
# the scripted hands put the blade's line is not measured.
# Control: two-hand-half-sword-off.txt (HalfSwording=0 refuses the blade).
# Run with: powershell -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\two-hand-half-sword.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands TwoHandGrip=1
ini Hands HalfSwording=1
ini Hands TwoHandReachUnits=60
console player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console player.equipitem 0001C6CD
console-at ready CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: the two-hander's model
expect Hands: the left hand took the two-handed sword's blade (half-swording)
expect Hands: the left hand drawn on the handle
expect Hands: the left hand slides along the handle with the trigger held
expect Hands: the left hand let go of the handle (the grip opened)
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Hands: the left grip closed off the handle
reject Hands: grab - left grip

wait 12
mark ready
wait 8
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn
left pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.62
wait 0.5
mark near_blade
left pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.70
wait 0.5
left grip 1
wait 1
mark on_blade
left trigger 1
left pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.85
wait 1
mark slid_up
left trigger 0
left grip 0
wait 1
mark let_go
