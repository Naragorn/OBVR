# The harness save keeps its Elven Longsword at the left hip, equipped. An Iron Longsword (00000C0C)
# equipped by the console goes to the right hip (the left one is taken), and the game hangs it there:
# the Elven sword is in the pack, kept at the left hip - OBVR hangs a copy of it there ("the left hip
# shows"). The Elven sword drawn at the left hip: it is equipped again, the game hangs it, OBVR's copy
# goes ("taken off") and the Iron sword, now in the pack, hangs at the right hip instead. Sheathed at the
# left hip again, both hips are filled. Switched off, OBVR's copy goes.
# The head looks down at each mark for the films: --record 1 shows the hips.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --record 1 tools/hand-scripts/kept-on-body.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands ShowKeptWeapons=1
# The reach opens the hand after a draw: WeaponHold (2026-10-24) would drop it.
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
ini Body Visible=1
# The films: the lower half of the eye, where the hips are.
ini Debug RecordCropTop=0.4
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console-at settle set timescale to 0
console-at settle set gamehour to 12
console-at settle player.equipitem 00000C0C
ini-at off Hands ShowKeptWeapons=0
expect HandScript: finished
expect Kept on body: the left hip shows
expect Kept on body: the right hip shows 00000C0C
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon
count 1 Kept on body: the copy is a

expect Kept on body: (mark drawn_left) the right hip shows 00000C0C
expect Kept on body: (mark both_sheathed) the right hip shows 00000C0C
expect Kept on body: the right hip - 00000000 off
expect Kept on body: the right hip's 00000C0C taken off - the hip wants another or none
