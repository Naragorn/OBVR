# A sword the hand let go of, pulled back by the grip (the tester's run of
# 2026-11-26 on Windows, the tutorial dungeon: his sword fell from the hand in
# a fight, and five grips at it on the floor each logged "reaching for
# something within 0.10 m" - no pull; Activate took it). weapon-pull.txt
# drops its sword by the console, this one by the hand as weapon-drop.txt
# does: drawn at the hip, let go of in front, then the weapon hand points down
# at it on the floor and closes - the sword has to float to the hand and be
# equipped ([Hands] PullToHand).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --no-steamvr tools/hand-scripts/weapon-drop-pull-back.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands GrabEquipsWeapons=1
ini Hands PullToHand=1
# The harness save's question box closed first.
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Weapon hold: let go - dropped where the hand is - the new reference
expect a grip pulls it to the hand
expect pulled to the hand and taken
expect Weapon grab: equipped and drawn - straight into the hand
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject NOT taken
reject stayed in the engine's grab

wait 20
mark settle
wait 1
mark sword_sheathed
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_drawn_held
right grip 0
wait 3
mark dropped
wait 6
head pitch -50
right pos 0.30 -1.05 -0.45
right rot -80 0 0
wait 2
mark near
right grip 0.9
wait 4
mark arrived
right grip 0
wait 2
mark drawn
wait 1
