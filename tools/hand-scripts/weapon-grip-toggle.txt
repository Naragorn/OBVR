# The grip toggle ([Hands] GripToggle=1, vr/GripToggle.h; Nadi 2026-11-25: "einmal grip reicht zum
# nehmen und waffe bleibt gehalten, wenn gehalten lässt grip die waffe wieder los"), short grips only
# (0.15 s, then open): a grip at the hip draws the sword and the hand keeps it with the grip open, three
# seconds out in front; a grip back at the hip sheathes it there; a grip there draws it again; a grip
# out in front drops it where the hand is. weapon-release-holster.txt is the same walk with the grip
# held, the toggle off.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --no-steamvr tools/hand-scripts/weapon-grip-toggle.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands GripToggle=1
# The harness save's question box (Lovers with PK.esm active without its esp, since 2026-10-31) closed first.
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
count 2 Grip toggle: the weapon hand took hold - kept closed until the next grip
count 2 Grip toggle: the weapon hand let go at the next grip
expect Weapon hold: the hold closed on the drawn weapon
expect Weapon hold: let go at the holster - sheathed
expect Weapon hold: let go - dropped where the hand is - the new reference
reject closed again - kept
reject lost what it kept
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20
mark settle
wait 1
mark sword_sheathed
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.15
right grip 0
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark held_with_the_grip_open
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.5
right grip 0.9
wait 0.15
right grip 0
wait 3
mark sheathed_by_a_grip
right grip 0.9
wait 0.15
right grip 0
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark drawn_again
right grip 0.9
wait 0.15
right grip 0
wait 3
mark dropped_by_a_grip
wait 1
