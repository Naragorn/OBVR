# A weapon kept at a hip and hung there by OBVR is taken at its hilt, wherever the places are
# (vr/SheathReach.h TakeKeptAtHilt; [Hands] ShowKeptWeapons; Nadi, 2026-11-16: "die ingame
# gezeichnete waffe am player body [muss] immer greifbar sein ... ob er nicht im holster spot ist").
# Every place moved down to the knees, as in sheath-grab.txt. The save's Elven Longsword is kept at
# the left hip; an Iron Longsword (00000C0C) equipped by the console goes to the right hip, so the
# Elven sword hangs at the left hip as OBVR's copy. A grip beside it takes nothing; a grip at its
# hilt equips and draws it.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/kept-on-body-hilt.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands ShowKeptWeapons=1
ini Body Visible=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
ini Hands HolsterOneHandUp=-1.10
ini Hands HolsterTwoHandUp=-1.10
ini Hands HolsterBowUp=-1.10
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.setav strength 100
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console-at settle player.equipitem 00000C0C
expect HandScript: finished
expect Sheath grab: (mark kept_on_body) the one-hander kept at the left hip hangs
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - equipping the one kept there, then drawing - taken at its hilt on the body
count 1 taken at its hilt on the body
expect Hands: ready weapon done
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
reject NOT hung
wait 20
mark settle
wait 3
head pitch -50
wait 1
mark kept_on_body
right pos -0.49 -0.57 0.00
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 1.5
mark grip_beside
right pos -0.19 -0.57 0.00
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 2.5
mark drawn
wait 0.5
