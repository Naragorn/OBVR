# Probe for free-weapons-spec 5.16 step 1: the left-handed mirror switched in a running game, the
# sword drawn and the shield on the arm. 5.16 wants the weapon handed to the left hand to switch the
# mirror on, and handed back to switch it off; this measures what the switch does today, through the
# setting's hot reload, before anything is built on it.
# The harness save's Elven Longsword kept at the left hip is drawn by the right hand, the Arena Iron
# Shield (000733ED) equipped by the console, a light swing with the right controller; then
# WeaponSide=2 (LeftHanded=1 before 2026-11-22): the skeleton should be drawn turned over (root determinant -1), the weapon hand the
# left controller, the shield in the right hand, and a light swing with the left controller should
# count; then WeaponSide=0 again. Read after the run: the films at each mark (the arms changing
# sides), and every line between the marks flip and flipped (the hold, the grab, the swing's history,
# the body IK's partners, the holster's watch).
# Does the body flicker at a switch (Nadi, 2026-11-20)? The head looks down at the body through both
# switches, the body is traced every frame (Debug.BodyTrace), and a 9 s film from every mark covers
# the run from settle on without a gap. Read with tools/drawn-arm-flicker.py, upper-body-flicker.py,
# draw-leg-flicker.py and film-flicker.py.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --no-steamvr --record 9 tools/hand-scripts/hand-side-switch-probe.txt

expect Hands: the other hand carries - shield

# Since step 3 the switch is the weapon's side alone ([Debug] WeaponSide), the controls stay
