# Opening by reaching ([Hands] ReachOpens, vr/ReachOpen.h): an empty chest
# (Oblivion.esm's ChestClutterMiddle01Empty, 000086C4) is placed 60 units
# in front of the player, the open right hand comes down onto it - no button
# pressed - and its menu opens over it; the hand taken away closes it, and
# the reach is armed again. Where the chest's lid is is not known to the
# script, so the hand comes down in steps until it is in the chest. Where
# the chest lands has changed: the first runs (2026-10-09, Windows) found it
# under the player - the CS wiki says PlaceAtMe falls back to the caller's
# own spot when the asked one is unsafe - and the hand went down at the
# feet; the Linux runs of 2026-10-25 found it where it was asked, its bound
# at 256 -1605 -258 for the eyes at 255 -1664 -144, its front 0.54 m ahead
# of a hand at the feet, and the hand never met it. The hand now comes down
# 0.7 m ahead, inside the chest's depth, and goes on forward at the bottom.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\reach-open.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=1
ini Look ContainerInWorld=1
console-at ready player.placeatme 000086C4 1 60 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect reached - activated
expect its menu opened
expect Container: the menu's panel on
expect degrees up to the eyes
expect the hand left - closed
expect the hand is away - armed again
expect top menu Container
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Reach: the close-all-menus function
reject another menu came

wait 10
mark ready
wait 4
head pitch -45
right pos 0.05 -0.40 -0.40
wait 1
mark above
right pos 0.05 -1.00 -0.70
wait 0.5
right pos 0.05 -1.20 -0.70
wait 0.5
right pos 0.05 -1.40 -0.70
wait 0.5
right pos 0.05 -1.58 -0.70
wait 0.5
right pos 0.05 -1.76 -0.70
wait 0.5
right pos 0.05 -1.76 -0.85
wait 0.5
right pos 0.05 -1.76 -1.00
wait 2
mark opened
right pos 0.30 -0.30 -0.10
wait 2
mark closed
wait 1
