# Probe for charged spells (controls-spec, "spells charged and released", step 1): how the player's cast moves
# through the caster's state, and where it is released (game/SpellRelease.h, the two sites in the animation
# update). Flare from the raised left hand: a short press, then the trigger held for 3 s. Read in the log: the
# "Spell release:" lines - the frames from the press to the release, the state before and after, and whether the
# held trigger changes anything (vanilla casts once per press).

ini Hands Enabled=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.addspell 000A97DF
console SetActiveSpell 000A97DF

expect HandScript: finished
expect Spell release: the call at 005FCE1D watched
expect Spell release: the call at 005FCF6E watched
expect Spell release: at
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark settle
wait 1
right pos 0.25 -0.60 -0.10
left pos -0.15 -0.30 -0.45
left rot 0 0 0
wait 1.5
mark tap
left trigger 1
wait 0.15
left trigger 0
wait 3
mark held
left trigger 1
wait 3
mark let-go
left trigger 0
wait 3
