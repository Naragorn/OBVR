# The stab that sticks (docs/blade-penetration-spec.md step 5; game/BladeStickLogic.h,
# game/BladeStick.cpp): the drawn longsword against a beggar with everything
# taken off (skin), his AI on, the player untouchable.
# - stand1: a stab at about 3 m/s into the chest goes in and sticks standing
#   ("stuck, standing"); then he is set on the player (startcombat) while the
#   hand holds the blade in - each second the log says how far he moved since
#   the stab ("moved ... units since the stab"); the hand draws the blade back

#   three seconds after is said ("moved ... in the 3 s after it was let go").
# - deep: a stab at about 10 m/s into the chest takes him down and he hangs on
#   the blade ("hanging in its ragdoll"); the hand moves sideways and back,
#   the body driven after it; drawn out.

#   with the blade in - now hanging on it"), drawn out.
# - load: the save made at the start loaded, a slow stab sticks, the save
#   loaded again with the blade in: let go without reading him ("let go - a
#   load" or "its bone no longer reads" when the weapon was put away first).
