# The body pressed against a wall and the head moved a little sideways (a Quest
# 3 tester, beta 8, 2026-11-06: "When I approach solid objects - like walls,
# tables, etc. - and press against them, turning my head (or taking a physical
# step to the side) causes the in-game character to move rapidly along the edge
# of the object I'm touching"). room-follow-wall.txt's place: the player set 80
# units before the hall's wall (setpos y -610, the wall at y about -529), the
# head pushed 1.5 m forward, so the feet stop at the wall and the rest stays a
# lean. Then the head goes 10 cm to the right (what a turn of the head moves the
# eyes), 30 cm (a step to the side), back to the middle, and to the left with a
# turn. A body that only follows the head moves its feet that far along the wall
# and stops; a body that slides runs on along the wall between the marks.
# Read from "RoomFollow: at the mark": the feet's x between a move's mark and the
# mark two seconds later must not change, and must have moved by the head's
# sideways move (about 7 units for 10 cm, 21 for 30 cm).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/room-follow-wall-slide.txt --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands RoomScaleBody=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console-at near player.setpos y -610

expect HandScript: finished
expect RoomFollow: at the mark - following
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 10
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
mark settle
wait 1.5
mark near
wait 2
mark start
head pos 0 1.6 -1.5
wait 2
mark pressed
wait 2
mark pressed-2
head pos 0.1 1.6 -1.5
wait 0.5
mark right10
wait 2
mark right10-2
head pos 0.3 1.6 -1.5
wait 0.5
mark right30
wait 2
mark right30-2
head pos 0 1.6 -1.5
wait 0.5
mark middle
wait 2
mark middle-2
head yaw 30
head pos -0.1 1.6 -1.5
wait 0.5
mark left10-turned
wait 2
mark left10-turned-2
head yaw 0
head pos 0 1.6 0
wait 2
mark back
wait 2
mark back-2
