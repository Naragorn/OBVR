# A weapon in the world taken at its grip (game/WeaponGrab.h, [Hands]
# GrabEquipsWeapons; free-weapons-spec section 5): an Iron Longsword dropped
# at the feet, the weapon hand brought to its grip point, the fingers closed
# (the hold by the pull, vr/GripHold.h) - taken into the inventory by the
# game's activation, equipped silently, drawn by the ready click once the
# game shows it. The "Weapon grab: the pick is on" line says how far the
# palm is from the grip, which is what set the hand's place below (the
# first run reads it, the second run proves it).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-grab.txt --run-timeout 120 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands GrabEquipsWeapons=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
# The pull off here: this run proves the take at the grip, weapon-pull.txt the pull.
ini Hands PullToHand=0
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.drop 00000C0C 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Weapon grab: the pick is on
expect a hold takes it
expect taken at its grip
expect Weapon grab: equipped - drawing
expect Hands: ready weapon done
expect Weapon grab: timeline - the weapon out of sight
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Weapon grab: given up
reject NOT taken
reject the weapon was not on the hand

# The dropped sword settles first: ten seconds were not enough for its
# physics (run 3, 21:03: it moved 11 cm while the hand stood at it).
wait 15
head pitch -50
# Run 2 (20:59) read the grip 0.10 left, 0.20 down and 0.04 back of the
# controller at 0.05 -1.40 -0.80: the hand goes there. The reach is
# measured at the controller, as every reach of the pick is.
# The hand closes on the way and arrives closed: the take comes all the
# same (the tester's third try of 2026-10-23 found no edge there).
right grip 0.9
right pos -0.05 -1.60 -0.76
right rot -80 0 0
wait 2
mark taken
right grip 0
wait 2
mark drawn
wait 1
