# Probe: does the console's equip put the Arena Iron Shield (000733ED) on
# the arm in the Linux install, with a sword in the slot? Four runs of
# 2026-10-24 never saw it worn: the harness save has nothing in the weapon
# slot (the fists), and the one equip tried with a sword had a torch in the
# other hand. Here: the Iron Longsword (00000C0C) added and equipped at the
# start, nothing in the other hand; the shield added and equipped at the
# ready mark; the sword drawn by the hip's reach; the shield equipped once
# more with the sword drawn. The "carries" line of HolsterShieldTorch
# reports every change of the worn shield and torch, the state line the
# slot at each mark.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/shield-equip-probe.txt --run-timeout 120 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C
console-at shield player.additem 000733ED 1
console-at shield player.equipitem 000733ED
console-at again player.equipitem 000733ED

expect HandScript: finished

wait 20
mark ready
wait 1
mark shield
wait 3
mark after_shield
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.5
right grip 0
wait 0.3
right pos 0.20 -0.45 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn
wait 1
mark again
wait 3
mark after_again
wait 1
