# The strike at the touch (Nadi, 2026-10-27: "Waffe schwingt oft durch
# gegner und erst dann kommt der hit ... anime style" - "go B"): every body
# the blade meets is struck in the frame it meets them - light, or a power
# attack when the hand is at [Hands] PowerStrikeSpeed (4.5 m/s) or the swing
# already one - never held to the swing's end (game::StrikeAtContact). The
# run is blade-slides-off.txt's: a frozen beggar, four slashes across them
