# A target spell from the casting hand (vr/SpellAim.h; Nadi, 2026-10-28):
# Flare (000A97DF, Fire Damage on Target, every player starts with it) made
# the active spell. The left hand hangs low - no crosshair; raised ahead - the
# crosshair on its laser; its trigger casts - the spell along the left hand's
# laser and from its palm ("Aim: the spell starts at the casting hand" says
# how far the engine's magic node stood from it). Then cast once more with
# the left hand turned 30 degrees in yaw: the turn in the "Spell:" line
# follows it. The eye pictures at each mark show the crosshair and the bolt.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\spell-casting-hand.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
console player.addspell 000A97DF
console SetActiveSpell 000A97DF

expect HandScript: finished
expect Spell: the active spell's reach target
expect crosshair on that hand on
expect Crosshair: the casting hand's laser meets
expect Aim: the spell starts at the casting hand
expect the aim along the laser
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the spell's start not moved

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.60 -0.10
left pos -0.25 -0.65 -0.05
left rot 0 0 0
wait 1.5
mark lowered
dump

wait 1.5
mark raised
dump
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
mark casting
dump
left trigger 0
wait 0.5
mark cast
dump
wait 2.5
left rot 0 30 0
wait 1
mark turned
dump
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 0.5
mark cast-turned
dump
