# The weapons' block by contact ([Hands] WeaponBlockByContact=1; vr/HandBlockLogic.h,
# game/ParryLogic.h ForceBlockForWeapon / ContactConeFor; Nadi, 2026-11-27, "ja go"):
# the guard pose of a drawn sword holds no Block; a blow is blocked when the
# blade is in its way at the blow (the weapon's angle rule, its numbers unchanged).
#  1. The Steel Longsword drawn by the holster (block-angle.txt's draw), the guard
#     held level across the body while the stick sprints forward 2 s: no
#     "blocking" line, "the weapon's guard is up - no block held", and a run's
#     ground covered (the walk a held block gives would not).
#  2. A bandit (00069AD1) placed, tgm, the head facing it (`head face 1`); 25 s
#     with the guard level across: its blows "a block forced for the weapon by
#     contact 1", "at the weapon by contact (no block held, the block forced for
#     the weapon) - the blades ... - blocked" when they cross.
#  3. 25 s with the blade held along the view (pointing at the bandit): the
#     blows "THROUGH, the guard stands wrong" - the blade is not in their way.
# Which verdicts come depends on the bandit's swings. Not run yet (written
# 2026-11-27 on Windows); the 400 units of the sprint are a guess (sprint.txt:
# 430 in 1 s at the sprint).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-block-contact.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands WeaponBlockByContact=1
ini Hands WeaponStopsAtBodies=1
ini Hands CombatReach=85
ini Hands BlockByAngle=1
ini Hands AnalogWalk=1
ini Hands ArmSwingRun=0
ini Hands ReachOpens=0
console-at ready player.additem 00000C0C 1
console-at ready player.equipitem 00000C0C
console-at placed player.placeatme 00069AD1 1
console-at placed tgm

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
expect Hands: the weapon's guard is up - no block held, the blade blocks what it is in the way of
expect AnalogWalk: sprinting
expect a block forced for the weapon by contact 1
expect at the weapon by contact (no block held, the block forced for the weapon)
expect - blocked
expect THROUGH, the guard stands wrong
reject Hands: the left hand is up - blocking
reject HandScript: WARNING
moved sprint_on sprint_end 400

wait 12
wait 1
mark ready
wait 4
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right pos 0.00 -0.45 -0.35
wait 2
mark drawn
# 1. The guard level across, sprinting by the stick.
right pos 0.10 -0.15 -0.40
right rot 0 90 0
wait 1
left stick 0 1
left click 1
wait 0.5
mark sprint_on
wait 2
mark sprint_end
left click 0
left stick 0 0
wait 1
# 2. The bandit; the guard level across.
mark placed
wait 3
head face 1
wait 25
mark level_done
# 3. The blade along the view.
right rot 0 0 0
wait 25
mark along_done
