# The main menu's buttons after a step in the room (Nadi in the headset,
# 2026-10-21: "wenn man einen schritt geht in vr die buttons nicht mehr
# gehen"). Since 2026-10-21 the main menu is delivered CinemaOverlay
# (FrameLogic.h): the eyes black on the held pose, the menu on the overlay
# quad the in-game menus hang on - a transform in tracking space that the
# compositor draws and the laser meets alike, so the beam's dot and the
# clicked pixel cannot part when the head moves (a cinema picture on a
# frozen pose was the compositor's to place, which the beam's arithmetic
# could not know). The quad hangs 1.2 m ahead, 1.6 m wide (the run of
# 2026-10-21 21:20: the beam at pitch 35 met it at pixel 1419,1282, below
# the row; pitch 41 is the row's middle by the two hits of that run and of
# main-menu-click.txt). The synthetic head steps - the hands hang from it,
# so the beam keeps its direction and moves over the quad like a pointer:
#   forward 0.3 m        Options still (the beam nearly level)
#   then 0.17 m right    Credits (Options' middle to Credits' is 0.17 m)
#   back to the left     Options
#   1 m right, away      the beam off the quad; after a second the flat
#                        picture is taken along and the quad with it
#                        (vr::FlatFollow), so the same aim is Options again
# and the trigger there must open the Options menu. Each place is held two
# seconds past its mark, so the picture (about 1.3 s after it) shows the
# button lit there - the game's own highlight, not only the tile in the log.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/main-menu-step.txt --save continue --run-timeout 150 --headless

start main-menu
ini Hands Enabled=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: at the mark - the laser hits the menu quad at pixel
count 4 the tile "main_options"
count 1 the tile "main_credits"
expect Render: the flat picture taken along, and the menu's quad with it
expect Hands: click sent to the Main menu
expect Hands: menu Options (0x3F7) - the hands go
reject the laser hits the flat picture
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 6
mark menu
right pos 0.15 -0.25 -0.35
right rot 41 0 0
wait 2
mark options-lit
head pos 0 1.6 -0.3
wait 3
mark stepped-forward
wait 2
head pos 0.17 1.6 -0.3
wait 3
mark stepped-right
wait 2
head pos 0 1.6 -0.3
wait 3
mark stepped-back
wait 2
head pos 1 1.6 -0.3
wait 4
mark followed
wait 2
right trigger 1
wait 0.3
right trigger 0
wait 2
mark options-open
wait 1
