# An arrow that sticks in the world, taken by the grip (game/StuckArrow.h; a
# Quest 2 tester, 2026-11-25: "Arrows stuck in training targets / wood etc
# cannot be recovered as they cannot be grabbed"). In the Chorrol Fighters
# Guild's basement, a step before its chained doll (0001DB3F): the bow drawn
# from behind the left shoulder, an arrow shot into the doll the vanilla way
# (the weapon hand's trigger held and let go, the bow by hand off), the weapon
# hand brought to the arrow and its grip closed. The arrow sticks in a hanging
# part ("Arrow take: at the mark arrow ... state 2, hit record there, kind 1:
# stuck"), the grip takes it ("taken where it stuck") and the cell has no
# arrow left. Before the fix the grip put the grab key down, the engine's grab
# refused the arrow's keyframed body ("the engine did NOT take it") and the
# arrow stayed.
# Where an arrow does not stick: shot into stone it bounces off and lies loose
# (kind 3) - the harness save's floor, this basement's walls - and the grab
# holds that one like any item.
# The guild's members turn on the scripted player in this cell; the scenario
# is over before that matters.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/arrow-take.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll
