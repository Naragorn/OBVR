# game dungeon deren texte zu ersetzen ... mit unseren vr controls"). The
# Tutorial quest's stage entries carry "&sUActnRdyitem;"-style tokens that
# the font's layout turns into "press F" through the formatter at 0057C240;
# detoured (game/ControlWords.h), the mode answers its own phrase. The save is
# the tester's Altmer in the Imperial Prison cell at the game's start
# (OBVRHarnessTall): setstage MQ01 28 raises the card "To ready your weapon
# or fists, ... To attack, ... To block, ..." with three tokens; xOBSE's
# CloseAllMenus puts it away (a card up holds the next quest update back, a
# stage the save has passed - 10, the walk - raises none, and Escape over

# then stage 34 (the cast and the journal) and stage 80 (the sneak). The
# card is the quest update's Generic menu (0x3F3), "Quest updated -
# Tutorial". Then two cards swapped whole (game/TutorialCards.h, step 2):
# stage 26 ("Shift-click to drop an item") and stage 76 (the hotkeys by a
# number key), the latter with a token of its own that step 1 answers.
