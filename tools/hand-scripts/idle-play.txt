# A scripted idle on the player in Full VR (Nadi, 2026-10-20: "mach
# Harness-Lauf mit player.pickidle" - what a dance or pose mod does): xOBSE's
# PlayIdle ("plays a specific idle on the calling actor", the second argument
# forcing it past the idle's conditions) and the game's own PickIdle, tried in
# turn with the head bowed 60 degrees onto the body - player.pickidle, then
# PlayIdle Speech_HandsInAir.kf (SheogorathPower, 0001742D) forced, then
# playidle FollowMe.kf (EscortWaitingWave, 000274C9) plain - then the game's
# own R (the view switch, no controller input is bound to it in Full VR) for
# third person with pickidle and DrinkBottleIdle.kf (TGDrinkSkooma, 0000CF50)
# there, then R back. The first run (13:31, PlayIdle forced alone) saw no
# special idle on either skeleton: "Hands: at the mark - the player's
# animation" has the five slots and the engine's own idle answer. Read: the pictures at the marks (the body
# under the head in first person, the whole body in third), "Body IK: at the
# mark - the right arm reaches its hand (the wrist N units off it ...)" - a
# wrist off the hand means the idle's animation landed after OBVR's arm
# writes - and "Camera: switched to ..." for the R key.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/idle-play.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless
