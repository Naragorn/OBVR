# The headset steering nothing ([Head] HeadLook=0, a Nexus request of
# 2026-10-19: the view by mouse or stick alone, the picture fixed in front of
# the face, to look at the floor for an item while seated): the seated mode
# (Full VR off), the synthetic head turned 45 degrees each way and bowed 30,
# then the game's own camera turned and tilted by the console (player.setangle
# z 90, x 30). With head look off the eyes' heading and pitch in "Camera: at
# the mark" must not follow the head and must follow the console; the control
# run headlook-on.txt shows the head taking the view as before. "Config:
# Head.HeadLook=0" says the setting and the unblocked vertical look took.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/headlook-off.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=0
ini Head HeadLook=0
console-at turned player.setangle z 90
console-at pitched player.setangle x 30
console-at straight player.setangle x 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Config: Head.HeadLook=0
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
head yaw 45
wait 2
mark head-left
head yaw -45
wait 2
mark head-right
head yaw 0
head pitch -30
wait 2
mark head-down
head pitch 0
wait 1
mark turned
wait 2
mark turned-2
mark pitched
wait 2
mark pitched-2
mark straight
wait 2
mark end
