# A spell's glow in the mirrored hand (free-weapons-spec 5.16, the torch's flame under the mirror:
# "other unmirrored effects with the hand - a spell's glow - should now draw too, not filmed").
# Left-handed: the first-person skeleton mirrored, the spell hand is the physical right one. Flare
# (000A97DF) charged by the right trigger held 2.5 s, then let go. Only a turned-over shape culls the
# other way under the mirror (render/ShapeTurnHook.h). The spell's glow ("PCloud Projectile Local")
# hangs under the mirrored hand, turned over, and was culled with the swap: in the first film
# (2026-11-26, 20261126-120301-slot1) the palm had no glow where the right-handed control's burns.
# A particle draw faces the camera whatever its turn and keeps the engine's cull since
# (render/BackfacePass.h DrawnShapeTurn).
