# A walker's grab from a real hit (Nadi, 2026-11-04: "go 1. also greifen die
# quasi normal an aber statt dmg und so halten die einen fest"): one walker
# placed near the player in god mode attacks as it will; with ChancePercent
# 100 in OblivionWalkers.ini (no TestGrabSeconds) a hit grabs instead of
# hurting, the walker lunges to the held forearm (OBVR's 'WGHP') and OBVR
# lays its hand on it ([Hands] WalkerGrip). Eye dumps every second while it
# holds. OblivionWalkers.log says each hit's distance and the lunge.
# Run: Walkers' INI backed up, [Grab] ChancePercent=100 appended, restored after.
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/walker-grip-real.txt --headless

ini Hands WalkerGrip=1
ini Debug EyeDump=1
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setangle z 0
console-at placed player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1 120 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect holds the player
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.15 -0.20 -0.35
left pos -0.15 -0.20 -0.35
wait 1
mark ready
wait 1
mark placed
wait 6
mark watch-1
wait 1
mark watch-2
wait 1
mark watch-3
wait 1

wait 1
mark watch-5
