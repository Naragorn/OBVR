# The two-hander taken from the shoulder with the fists up (docs/weapon-collision-spec.md, "a two-hander
# with no blade"; Nadi, Windows headset 2026-11-15: no strike landed until another weapon was drawn).
# With the fists up the weapon state is already Drawn, so the claymore's shape is read in the frame of its
# equip - through world data its model did not have yet, 1016 units long; now in the node's own space.
# The claymore in the slot, the weapon hand closed (the sheathed weapon taken off for the fists), then,
# the fist still closed, the grip over the right shoulder: the claymore equipped and drawn at once. Its
# shape must read as it does in any other frame (78.6 units on the Iron Claymore), and the strikes arm.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\two-hander-from-fists.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands Fists=1
ini Hands FittedWeaponShapes=1
# The reach opens the hand after a draw: WeaponHold (2026-10-24) would drop it.
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.setav strength 100
console player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console player.equipitem 0001C6CD
expect HandScript: finished
expect Fist: the weapon hand closed - the sheathed weapon taken off for the fists
expect Holster: reach for the two-handed weapon - equipping the one kept there
expect Contact: the weapon 0001C6CD's shape - 780 vertices, its tip 78.6 units out
expect Hands: strikes by motion armed - weapon type 1
reject its tip 1016
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
# The scripted weapon hand is an Index: its fist is its fingers (vr::FistFingersOf).
right index 1
wait 20
mark settle
wait 1
mark claymore_in_slot
# The fist: the sheathed claymore off, the fists up.
right curl 1
wait 2
mark fists_up
# The fist still closed, over the right shoulder: the claymore equipped and drawn.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark claymore_drawn
wait 1
