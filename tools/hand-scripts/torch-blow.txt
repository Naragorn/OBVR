# The torch's blow (game/TorchBlowLogic.h; Nadi, 2026-10-26: "Mit torch ist
# es aktuell zu einfach gegner zu boden zu hauen ... fackel hit zwar npcs
# recoiled aber nicht gleich umhaut. umhauen erst mit ordentlich schwung whl
# soviel wie der schwerer angriff hat beim fighten"). An Iron Longsword
# (00000C0C) drawn by the holster, Torch02 (0002CF9F) in the left hand, a
# beggar placed 60 units ahead and frozen (tai); his INI's shove speeds
# (ShoveSpeed 1.5, ShoveHardSpeed 2.4). The torch hand slashes across the
# beggar twice from his right, both far past the hard shove's speed:
#   0.7 m  - a light swing: held to its end, then staggered
#   1.6 m  - met 0.8 m in, the swing a power one after: knocked down
# The hand on the torch is left open (curl 0.8, under the fist's 0.9): the
# torch's blow is a blow all the same, never a slap in the face (the first
# run's slash slapped him, and Put it in its Place's slap moved him off).
# torch-blow-speed.txt is the same with TorchKnockdownPowerOnly=0, the
# speed alone (the first slash knocks him down there).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/torch-blow.txt --direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands OffHandStrikes=1
ini Hands OffHandGrabs=0
ini Hands TwoHandOneHanders=0
ini Hands PushPeople=0
ini Hands TorchDrop=0
ini Hands ShoveSpeed=1.5
ini Hands ShoveHardSpeed=2.4
ini Hands SwingLight=2.6
ini Hands SwingMinMetres=0.20
ini Hands PowerSwingMetres=1.5
ini Hands PowerThrustMetres=0.6
# The stagger's push off, so the second slash finds him where he stood.
ini Hands ShoveDistance=0
ini Hands TorchKnockdownPowerOnly=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C
console player.additem 0002CF9F 1
console-at ready player.equipitem 0002CF9F
console-at ready player.placeatme 0001C45B 1 60 0
console-at ready tai

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
count 2 the torch's blow held to the swing's end
expect its swing ended short of a power one: staggered
expect its swing grew into a power one: knocked down
reject the torch, by the speed alone
reject in the face
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
left curl 0.8
left rot 0 0 0
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.20
right rot 0 0 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 26
mark placed
# The sword drawn from the hip (weapon-drop.txt's draw) and held low at the
# right side, pointing back, the grip kept closed (WeaponHold lets go of an
# opened one).
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
right rot 0 180 0
right pos 0.25 -0.55 0.05
wait 1
mark drawn
# The torch hand taken round to the beggar's right, well clear of him: out
# to the side first, then forward.
left pos 0.90 -0.35 -0.20
wait 0.6
left pos 0.90 -0.35 -0.80
wait 1.0
mark aside
# Blow 1: one quick slash across, 0.7 m - short of a power swing (1.5 m) -
# ending before his middle (past it the hand moves away from him).
left pos 0.20 -0.35 -0.80
wait 1.0
mark light
left pos 0.90 -0.35 -0.80
wait 3.0
mark back
# Blow 2: the same slash carried on, 1.6 m in four steps - it meets him
# 0.8 m into the swing and becomes a power swing after.
left pos 0.50 -0.35 -0.80
wait 0.02
left pos 0.10 -0.35 -0.80
wait 0.02
left pos -0.30 -0.35 -0.80
wait 0.02
left pos -0.70 -0.35 -0.80
wait 1.5
mark power
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.20
wait 1.5
mark end
