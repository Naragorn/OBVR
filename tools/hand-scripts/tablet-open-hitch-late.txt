# tablet-open-hitch.txt with [Render] LatePoseWait=1 (vr/PosePrediction.h):
# the update on predicted poses, the wait at the render's entry.
#
# The frame the Tab menus open on, on the tablet (docs/inventory-tablet-spec.md
# 4.5 and 5: "the hitch when the Tab menu opens", never measured). The left B
# opens and closes the tablet five times, the world running behind it; a
# profiler capture with the render sampler over all of it. Marks: idle (a
# steady stretch), pre-N before the press, open-N 1.5 s after it, held-N
# before the close, shut-N 1.5 s after it - the phases pre-N to open-N hold
# the opening frame, held-N to shut-N the closing one. Read with
# tools/perf-phases.py <run> idle pre1 open1 held1 shut1 pre2 ... end and
# tools/perf-samples.py for where the long frames went. The control is
# tablet-open-hitch-off.txt (the menus on the big quad, the world paused).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/tablet-open-hitch-late.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --run-timeout 300
