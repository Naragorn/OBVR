# A severed head keeps its face, and a cut is kept when the corpse is stripped (docs/dismemberment-spec.md,
# "2026-11-15: heads of hair and teeth, the stretch at the neck"; game/CutSideLogic.h).
# dismember-corpse.txt's bandit, its head cut by [Debug] TestCut at the mark "cut". Then:
#   * two seconds of rescans (one every 0.5 s): FaceGenFace, the head's at the cut, must not be taken for
#     the body's again - "FaceGenFace(body" was the face collapsed into the stump, the hair and the teeth
#     alone on the ground;
#   * the corpse stripped (removeallitems): the shape that held the cut's check is gone with the clothes,
#     the face still points at the cut's nodes - the cut must not be forgotten (its nodes zeroed under the
#     face: the stretch from the neck), and the naked body's new shape must be swapped by the next rescan.
#     Whether the check is seen gone depends on the freed bone list being used again by then (run of
#     2026-11-16: it still held its value, the cut simply stood) - so "the cut kept and its skins read
#     again" is not expected here; its decision is dismember_test's (CutFateFor).
# Without the lines for Oblivion Walkers that dismember-corpse.txt expects (its DLL may be off).
