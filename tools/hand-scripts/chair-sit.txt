# Sitting on a chair in Full VR: the engine switches to third person for the
# sit-down (Enhanced Camera's one hook at 0x0066C63D - "chair, fatigue
# knockout, paralysis") and stays there for the whole of the sitting, back by
# itself at the get-up. Beta 5 held first person off only while the sit-down
# byte (+0x71C) was set and would have forced first person once seated; on
# 2026-10-20 the sit/sleep state (+0x11D: 3 sitting down, 4 seated, 5 getting
# up) held it too, and by 12:50 the force itself was gone - the engine undoes
# every switch of its own. Aelwin Merowald's house
# in Weye (coc Weye): the player 70 units behind and 40 beside the chair
# 00095BC3 (LCChair02L at 210 -2 -42, rotZ 1.05), faced towards it, the head
# bowed 35 degrees onto it, A sits. Marks seated with head turns, then A
# again stands the player up (the left stick forward did not, 12:01 - the
# eyes stayed on the chair), the stick walks after. "Hands: the engine's third person
# left alone - sit/mount bytes ..., sit/sleep state ..., knock state ..."
# says what held it, "Camera: switched to third/first person (frame N, ...)"
# the engine's own switches (and the only ones, Full VR forces nothing since
# 2026-10-20), "Camera: at the mark" the eyes.
