# The weapon hand's trigger with the weapon away (vr/AttackControl.h; a Quest 2
# tester, 2026-11-25: "Stop equipped weapon popping into hand when right
# trigger is pressed"). The sword equipped and sheathed, the trigger pulled a
# second and a half: the attack key stays up and the sword stays at the hip.
# Then a Touch controller's fist - the trigger and the grip pulled, the
# fingers curled: the sheathed sword is taken off and the fists come up
# (vr/Fist.h). Before the fix the trigger's attack drew the sword ("weapon 2
# (slot 1)" in the state line, "attack key 1" in the items line), and the
# fist was then the hand round it.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/trigger-weapon-away.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Fists=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect weapon 1 (slot 1)
reject weapon 2 (slot 1)
reject attack key 1
expect Fist: the weapon hand closed - the sheathed weapon taken off for the fists
expect Fist: the slot is empty - fists up
expect weapon 2 (slot 0)
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark sheathed
right trigger 1
wait 0.5
mark trigger_held
wait 1.0
right trigger 0
wait 2
mark trigger_let_go

# A Touch controller's fist: the trigger, the grip, the fingers curled.
right trigger 1
right grip 1
right curl 1
wait 3
mark fist
right trigger 0
right grip 0
right curl 0
wait 1.5
mark open
