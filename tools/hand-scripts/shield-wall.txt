# The worn shield stops at walls ([Hands] HandsStopAtWalls,
# game/HandContactLogic.h, game/ShieldShape.cpp): a shield is put on the
# left arm and a longsword drawn by the holster - sword and shield, the case
# the tester asked for first. The shield's disc is read from its model
# ("Contact: the shield's shape"), then the left arm is lowered step by step

# the drawn end of the disc's first spoke.
