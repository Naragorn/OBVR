# Head turns WHILE the horse walks (Nadi in the headset, 2026-10-19 evening:
# "looking right or left while riding does not turn the view"): the mount of
# horse-ride-turnprobe.txt (the AI held off for the aim, on for the press, off
# again once mounted), then the left stick forward and, with the horse
# walking, the head turned 45 degrees each way. The harness's standing turns
# turned the view fully (runs of 2026-10-19); the walk is the difference - the
# walk direction's steering (vr/WalkDirection.h) writes the player's heading
# towards the head while the stick walks, and on a horse the engine holds the
# player's rotZ at the horse's heading. "Camera: at the mark" gives the eyes'
# heading at ride-left/ride-right against ride-ahead; "Aim: a written heading
# did not land" and "Walk: the body turns" say whether the steering wrote.
# Debug.HorseTurnProbe=1 adds the per-frame riding trace (the engine's camera
# over the horse while it moves) for the bob of the walk. The AI is switched
# back on at yaw-ahead: with it off the horse does not walk on the stick
# (horse-ride-turnprobe.txt, 13:01 - the eyes 0.1 units from the mount at "riding").
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/horse-ride-steer.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug HorseTurnProbe=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=0
console-at ready player.moveto 0002008C
console-at ready SetNumericINISetting "fAutoVanityModeDelay:Camera" 100000
console-at priory player.moveto 0002008C 0 -100 0
console-at priory player.setangle z 0
console-at priory tai
console-at aimed tai
console-at mounted tai
console-at yaw-ahead tai

expect HandScript: finished
expect Camera: the camera node's ancestry
expect the player has mounted
expect Camera: riding at the mark
expect a world frame while the game loads
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 30
mark priory
wait 4
head yaw 0
head pitch -20
right pos 0.15 -0.35 -0.35
right rot -5 0 0
wait 2
mark aimed
right a 1
wait 0.3
right a 0
wait 3
mark pressed
head pitch 0
wait 12
mark mounted
wait 5
mark mounted-5
wait 5
mark mounted-10
head yaw 45
wait 3
mark yaw-left
head yaw -45
wait 3
mark yaw-right
head yaw 0
wait 2
mark yaw-ahead
left stick 0 1
wait 4
mark ride-ahead
head yaw 45
wait 3
mark ride-left
head yaw -45
wait 3
mark ride-right
head yaw 0
wait 3
mark ride-ahead-2
left stick 0 0
wait 3
mark stopped
