# The cast on a button (OBVR_Controls.ini [Buttons] Cast, vr/ButtonBindings.h;
# a Quest 2 tester, 2026-11-19 and again 2026-11-25 with beta 9: "My left
# trigger casts spells and cant be rebound even with ... Cast=LeftA"). Flare
# (000A97DF) the active spell, the overlay's [Buttons] puts the cast on the
# other hand's A. The other hand's trigger pulled twice casts nothing; its A
# held and let go casts once. With a DLL that does not know the key (beta 9)
# the trigger casts twice and the A never.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/cast-on-button.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Buttons Cast=LeftA
console player.addspell 000A97DF
console SetActiveSpell 000A97DF

expect HandScript: finished
expect Cast=LeftA
expect Buttons: Cast from the left A
count 1 Aim: the spell starts at the casting hand
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.60 -0.10
left pos -0.15 -0.30 -0.45
left rot 0 0 0
wait 1.5
mark raised
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 2.5
mark trigger_once
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 2.5
mark trigger_twice
left a 1
wait 0.6
left a 0
wait 2.5
mark button
