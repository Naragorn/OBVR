# A crowd of Walkers in three sizes, for measuring what a person costs and
# which optimisation pays (Nadi, 2026-10-27: "bau ein harness mit 25, 50 und
# 100 walkers"): the prison of ImperialDungeon01 empty for fifteen captured
# seconds, then twenty-five Walkers placed at the player (player.placeatme
# 11000801 25), their AI switched off once (tai is global), fifteen seconds
# watched; twenty-five more, fifteen seconds; fifty more, fifteen seconds -
# 25, 50 and 100 standing in one heap at the player. One capture of 95 s
# (StartAfterSeconds 55) holds all four phases. perf-phases.py between the
# marks: "prison" to "empty-watched", "crowd25-placed" to "crowd25-watched",
# "crowd50-placed" to "crowd50-watched", "crowd100-placed" to
# "crowd100-watched"; the sampler says whose the time is (perf-samples.py).
# The heap is not a battle: no AI, no fighting, every body in view at once -
# the render cost of a person, nothing else. The quiet twin with eighteen is
# perf-walkers-standing.txt.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/perf-walkers-crowd.txt --headless --save OBVRHarness --run-timeout 420

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=0
ini Render Stereo=dual
ini Performance Enabled=1
ini Performance GpuTiming=0
ini Performance StartAfterSeconds=55
ini Performance CaptureSeconds=95
ini Performance MaxRecords=262144
ini Performance Sampler=1
console-at placed tgm
console-at placed coc ImperialDungeon01
console-at prison tcl
console-at prison player.setrestrained 1
console-at prison SetNumericINISetting "fVanityModeAutoDelay:Camera" 100000
console-at crowd25 player.placeatme 11000801 25
console-at crowd25-placed tai
console-at crowd50 player.placeatme 11000801 25
console-at crowd100 player.placeatme 11000801 50

expect HandScript: finished
expect Performance: capture armed, it starts in 55 seconds
expect Performance: capture 1 started
expect Performance: export complete
reject Performance: export partial
reject menu Container
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 22
mark placed
wait 16
mark prison
wait 20
mark empty-watched
mark crowd25
wait 8
mark crowd25-placed
wait 15
mark crowd25-watched
mark crowd50
wait 8
mark crowd50-placed
wait 15
mark crowd50-watched
mark crowd100
wait 10
mark crowd100-placed
wait 15
mark crowd100-watched
wait 8
mark done
