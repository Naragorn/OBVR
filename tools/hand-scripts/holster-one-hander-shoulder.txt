# A one-handed weapon kept over the shoulder (free-weapons-spec 5.12.3, the tester's wish of
# 2026-11-26): the sword drawn at the left hip, sheathed over the right shoulder - kept there, the
# hip's sheath hidden (the shoulder shows no model) - drawn from the shoulder again as from a hip,
# then sheathed at the hip: the hip's sheath shows again.
# The harness save keeps its Elven Longsword at the left hip, equipped.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --no-steamvr --parallel tools/hand-scripts/holster-one-hander-shoulder.txt --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
# The reach opens the hand after a draw: WeaponHold (2026-10-24) would drop it.
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
ini Body Visible=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console-at settle set timescale to 0
console-at settle set gamehour to 12

expect HandScript: finished
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - drawing (the weapon hand at the left hip
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - sheathing (the weapon hand at the right shoulder
expect Body: the sheath "SideWeapon" hidden - the equipped one-hander is kept at a shoulder
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - drawing (the weapon hand at the right shoulder
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - sheathing (the weapon hand at the left hip
expect Body: the sheath "SideWeapon" shown again
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
reject does not fit

wait 20
mark settle
wait 1

# The left hip: the sword drawn.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn_from_hip

# The right shoulder: sheathed and kept there.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark kept_at_shoulder
head pitch -80
wait 1
mark hip_no_sheath
head pitch 0
wait 0.5

# The same shoulder: the sword drawn again, as from a hip.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn_from_shoulder

# The left hip: sheathed there, the hip's sheath back.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 0.10 -0.50
wait 3
mark kept_at_hip
wait 1
