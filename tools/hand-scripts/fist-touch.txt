# The fist on a controller that is not an Index (vr/Fist.h FistFingersOf and
# FistKeepsGripFromGrab; Nadi, 2026-11-25: "trigger gehalten zählt bei quest
# als faust rdy. loslassen faust weg"). The scripted weapon hand is no Index,
# as a Touch is not. An apple dropped at the feet, the hand brought down to
# it. First the trigger and the grip together, a Touch fist: the fist by the
# trigger takes the harness save's sheathed sword off and raises the fists,
# and the grip at the apple grabs nothing. Then the trigger let go, the grip
# still closed: the fists go down and the grip takes the apple. Before, a
# Touch fist was the grip squeezed with the trigger, and the grip grabbed
# whatever the fist met (a Quest 2 tester's log of 2026-11-19: "grab - right
# grip took ... key 5A down" and "Fist: the weapon hand closed" together) -
# the build before the fix takes the apple into the fist at the first mark.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/fist-touch.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Fists=1
ini Hands StowAtBody=0
ini Hands EatAtMouth=0
ini Hands PushPeople=0
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.additem 0003365D 1
console player.drop 0003365D 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Fist: the weapon hand closed by its trigger - the sheathed weapon taken off for the fists
expect Fist: the slot is empty - fists up
expect Fist: the weapon hand's grip kept from the grab - that hand is a fist by its trigger
expect Fist: the weapon hand opened - fists down
count 1 grip took
expect Hands: holding
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 15
mark settle
wait 1
head pitch -50
right pos 0.00 -1.22 -0.73
right rot -80 0 0
wait 1.5
mark near
# A Touch fist: the trigger and the grip - and the fingers as SteamVR reads
# them from those two on a Touch (the index from the trigger, the other three
# from the grip), so the build before the fix makes its fist here too.
right trigger 1
right grip 1
right curl 1
wait 3
mark fist
# Let go of the trigger, the grip still closed (the three fingers on it stay
# curled; the script has one curl for all five, so it stays at 1).
right trigger 0
wait 3
mark grip_only
right grip 0
wait 1.5
mark open
