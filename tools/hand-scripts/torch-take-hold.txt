# A torch taken from the hip is held by the hold that took it (vr::HolsterVerdict
# leftTook; Nadi, Windows 2026-10-27: "wenn einmal ausgerüstet dann am
# controller Hold halten (oder sie geht in die spielwelt) statt toggle grip
# press"). Torch02 (0002CF9F) equipped by the console, stowed at the right hip
# by opening the hand there; the other hand closes at the right hip again and
# takes it back, carries it forward still closed, and opens: the torch falls,
# burning - no second closing needed. Before the fix the take's claim kept the
# torch's release from arming, and opening left it in the hand.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/torch-take-hold.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands HolsterShieldTorch=1
ini Hands TorchDrop=1
console player.additem 0002CF9F 1
console player.equipitem 0002CF9F

expect HandScript: finished
expect Torch: the hold closed on the torch - letting go drops it, burning
expect Torch: opened at a place on the body - stowed there
expect Holster: reach for the torch - taken back
expect Torch: let go - the new reference
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left grip 0.9
wait 1
mark held
left pos 0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.5
left grip 0
wait 2
mark stowed
left grip 0.9
wait 1.5
mark taken
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
left grip 0
wait 2
mark let_go
wait 1
