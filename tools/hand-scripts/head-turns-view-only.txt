# The headset turning the view only ([Look] HeadTurnsViewOnly; a Nexus
# user's request of 2026-11-02): seated (Hands Enabled=0), the mouse - here
# the console's player.setangle - turns and tilts the view and the aim; the
# head turns and tilts the view on top, the aim not. Read at each mark from
# "Free aim: at the mark - on; the view turned A tilted B, the aim turned C
# tilted D, the crosshair from the head x y z, the pick along the aim":
# - ready: view = aim, the crosshair straight ahead (0 0 -1);
# - head-left (head yaw 45): the view 45 further left, the aim unchanged, the
#   crosshair off to the right (x about 0.7);
# - head-down (head pitch -30): the view 30 lower, the aim level, the
#   crosshair above (y about 0.5);
# - pitched (setangle x 20, the aim 20 down) with the head 30 left: the view
#   tilted 20 down too and 30 left, the aim 20 down - the mouse's tilt reaches
#   the view (no BlockVerticalLook) and the horizon stays level.
# Run with: powershell -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\head-turns-view-only.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=0
ini Head HeadLook=1
ini Look HeadTurnsViewOnly=1
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready player.setangle x 0
console-at pitch player.setangle x 20
console-at end player.setangle x 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Config: Look.HeadTurnsViewOnly=1 - the headset turns the view only
expect Free aim: at the mark - on
expect the pick along the aim
reject Free aim: at the mark - off
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 1
mark level
head yaw 45
wait 2
mark head-left
head yaw 0
head pitch -30
wait 2
mark head-down
head pitch 0
wait 1
mark pitch
wait 2
head yaw 30
wait 2
mark pitched
head yaw 0
wait 1
mark end
wait 2
