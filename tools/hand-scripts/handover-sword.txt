# Handing the sword to the other hand and taking it back (game/HandoverLogic.h,
# free-weapons-spec 5.14; Nadi, 2026-10-28): the Iron Longsword (00000C0C)
# drawn at the right hip, the left hand closed on its blade, the weapon hand
# opened - the left hand holds it as an object ("Handover: ... handed over",
# "in the other hand's grab"); moved with the left hand; then the weapon
