# The view stays where it stood as the tablet's menus come and go (Nadi in
# the headset, 2026-11-27: "at inventory open the vr view and body take back
# one step. closing inv moves forward"; docs/inventory-tablet-spec.md 28).
# Under the tablet's menus the world runs on and the camera pass with it; the
# standing eye's hold was "not live" under a menu and passed the engine's
# first-person eye as it was. It keeps the eye's place over the feet now
# (camera/StandingEyeHold.h) and says, once the menu is gone, how far the
# engine's eye stood from it meanwhile - the measurement no run had taken.
#
# The run: the hands held away from the arms' rest pose (the first-person
# root is placed with the right hand, and Camera01 hangs under that root - the
# suspected way the engine's eye moves under a menu, not proved), a mark in
# the world, the left B opens the inventory on the tablet, a mark, the right
# hand moved 30 cm, a mark, the left B closes it, a mark.
#
# To read: "Camera: standing eye at the mark" at the four marks - the held
# eye over the feet is the same at all four ("held, standing" in the world,
# "kept under a menu" under it), whatever the engine's eye beside it reads;
# "Camera: at the mark - the eyes at ..." likewise the same place at all four; and the line
# "Camera: under a menu with the world running the view was kept ..." with
# how far the engine's eye stood off and from which frame.
# Before the change: the mark under the menu says "not held", and its eyes
# are where the engine's eye is.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/tablet-open-step.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands WalkDirection=head
ini Tablet Enabled=1
ini Tablet InScene=1
ini Render UnpausedMenus=0
ini Render MonitorMenusOnly=0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Tablet: in the off hand, drawn into the eyes
expect Camera: standing eye at the mark - kept under a menu
expect Camera: under a menu with the world running the view was kept where it stood
expect Tablet: closed - the menus' fade kept out of the room and the eyes
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
left pos -0.12 -0.32 -0.32
left rot 0 0 0
right pos 0.20 -0.45 -0.10
right rot 40 32 0
wait 2.5
mark world
wait 8
left b 1
wait 0.2
left b 0
wait 2
mark open
wait 8
right pos 0.20 -0.30 -0.40
wait 1.5
mark moved
wait 8
left b 1
wait 0.2
left b 0
wait 2.5
mark closed
wait 1
