# Enemy attacks half as often ([Hands] EnemyAttackFrequency=50,
# game/AttackTempo.h, combat-comfort-spec 4 item 2): one melee bandit
# (00069AD1) at an untouchable player (tgm), the ring off. The bandit's
# melee decisions fetch its combat style first (the scope), then roll
# against half its attack chance, and a decision to hold draws twice the
# hold: the log's "a hold of ... drawn between ... held for ... (x2.00)"
# lines, the state lines' count of holds drawn. The attack actions begun
# over the minute of marks, against attack-tempo-vanilla.txt's, are the
# measure.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\attack-frequency.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands CombatRing=0
ini Hands EnemyAttackSpeed=100
ini Hands EnemyAttackFrequency=50
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 1
console-at ready tgm

expect HandScript: finished
expect Attack tempo: Actor::GetCombatStyle detoured at 005E0F50
expect Attack tempo: the hold timer's draw (melee decision A) - the call at 00619176
expect held for
expect (x2.00)
expect speed x1.00, chance x0.50, hold x2.00 (on)
expect swings begun
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 10
mark fight1
wait 10
mark fight2
wait 10
mark fight3
wait 10
mark fight4
wait 10
mark fight5
wait 10
mark fight6
