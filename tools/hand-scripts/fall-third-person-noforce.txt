# fall-third-person.txt with [Hands] ForceFirstPerson=0 (Nadi, 2026-10-20: "was passiert
# wenn nutzer force 1st aus hat"): whether the engine comes back to first
# person by itself after the falls when Full VR never forces it, and what the
# view is left in when it does not. The expects say only what must happen
# either way; the switches are read off the log.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/fall-third-person-noforce.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands ForceFirstPerson=0
ini Look Exhaustion=0
console-at ready stopquest MQ01
console-at placed player.setav paralysis 1
console-at paralysed player.setav paralysis 0
console-at recovered player.forceav fatigue -100
console-at low player.forceav fatigue 145

expect HandScript: finished
expect Camera: switched to third person (frame
expect the engine's third person left alone
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the player's paralysis fall was skipped
reject the player's fatigue collapse was skipped

wait 12
right rot 0 0 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 3
mark placed
wait 3
mark down-3
wait 3
mark down-6
head yaw 45
wait 2
mark down-left
head yaw -45
wait 2
mark down-right
head yaw 0
wait 1
mark paralysed
wait 6
mark up-6
wait 4
mark recovered
wait 4
mark collapsed-4
wait 4
mark collapsed-8
mark low
wait 6
mark fit-6
head yaw 45
wait 2
mark fit-left
head yaw 0
wait 2
mark fit-10
