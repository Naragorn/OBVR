# Ultimate Leveling's text line over the bars as its own Hand HUD element

# nur an der hand rest noch im screen"): with Ultimate Leveling installed the
# line "hudmain_xp_description" is lifted whole to the left hand, apart from

# writes each element's lifted rectangle at the marks.

ini Hands Enabled=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect - XpText left, lifted
