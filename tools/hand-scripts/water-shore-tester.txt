# Water reflections from a Quest 3 tester's place (his video of 2026-10-21:
# the rocky north shore of Lake Rumare by the Talos Bridge's west end, a
# bridge pier close on the right, the city across the water - "the
# reflections are now low-quality and jittery"): the player put into the
# lake under the bridge (cow Tamriel 1 15, in the water at 5397,65496) and
# moved 900 units north onto that shore (player.setpos y 66400), the head
# turned round in steps of 30 degrees with a picture at each, as
# water-seam.txt does. What it proves: the capture is scaled from the eyes'

# reflection's texels against the runs of 2026-10-19 - no seam, no flat
# colour, no streaks anywhere round.
