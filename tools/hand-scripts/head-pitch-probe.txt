# The synthetic head's pitch against the picture (the harness, 2026-10-11:
# the body probe's pictures did not match the camera's logged pitch - a
# level camera showed the floor, -80 the ceiling). The head is pitched and
# held for six seconds before each mark, so neither easing nor the runner's
# reaction time can be the difference; the "Camera: at the mark" line gives
# the camera's own pitch at each mark, the picture is read against it.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\head-pitch-probe.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Camera: at the mark
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.30 -0.35
left pos -0.25 -0.30 -0.35
head pitch -60
wait 6
mark down60
head pitch 0
wait 6
mark level
head pitch 40
wait 6
mark up40
head pitch -85
wait 6
mark down85
head pitch 0
wait 3
mark end
