# Oblivion Walkers' disguise by hand (vr/WalkersDisguise.h, camera/hands/WalkersDisguise.h;
# Walkers 4.20.1, 475c3f3): a walker placed ahead and killed, the player
# sneaking (Walkers asks for it), the right hand put into the body and then
# on the chest. Seen: 'WGBL' with the body taken by Walkers, Walkers' 'WGDS' 1
# back, the black-red vignette and the darker view fading in, blood laid on
# the hands; with Walkers' [Disguise] Seconds=40 (tools/walkers-disguise-run.sh
# sets it for the run) 'WGDS' 0 and the look fading out.
# The hand is far from a lying body's trunk on a script's fixed pose, so the
# reach is widened for the run ([Hands] DisguiseBodyReachUnits); the
# gesture's own rules are walkers_disguise_test's. The vignettes are SteamVR
# overlays and do not show in the game's pictures; the hands' blood would.
# Run with: bash tools/walkers-disguise-run.sh

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands WalkersDisguise=1
ini Hands DisguiseBodyReachUnits=140
ini Render MonitorMenusOnly=0
ini Debug EyeDump=1
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setangle z 0
console-at placed player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1 80 0
console-at kill {actor}.kill
console-at sneak player.setforcesneak 1
console-at end player.setforcesneak 0

expect HandScript: finished
expect Walkers: 'WGVR' 1 taken
expect Walkers: 'WGBL'
expect taken - a dead body's blood smeared over the chest
expect Walkers: 'WGDS' 1
expect Walkers disguise: on
expect Walkers disguise: blood at the right hand
expect Walkers disguise: blood laid on the hands
expect Walkers: 'WGDS' 0
expect Walkers disguise: over
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 0.5
mark placed
wait 1
mark kill
wait 2
mark sneak
wait 1
head pitch -45
right pos 0.05 -1.30 -0.70
wait 0.6
mark in_body
right pos 0.05 -0.60 -0.40
wait 0.3
head pitch 0
right pos 0.03 -0.30 -0.18
wait 0.5
mark at_chest
wait 2
head pitch -50
right pos 0.10 -0.35 -0.35
left pos -0.10 -0.35 -0.35
wait 1
mark hands_bloody
wait 1
head pitch 0
wait 58
mark after
wait 3
mark end
wait 1
