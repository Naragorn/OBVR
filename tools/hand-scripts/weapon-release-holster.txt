# The drawn weapon let go of at a holster and at the chest (game/WeaponRelease.h;
# free-weapons-spec section 5, decisions 3, 10 and 11): the sword drawn by the
# hold closing at the hip, held out in front, brought back to the hip and let
# go - sheathed, not dropped; drawn again, brought to the chest spot (0.17 m
# ahead, 0.33 m under the eyes) and let go - into the inventory, unequipped.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-release-holster.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
expect Weapon hold: the hold closed on the drawn weapon
expect Weapon hold: let go at the holster - sheathed
expect Weapon hold: let go at the chest - into the inventory
reject dropped where the hand is
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20
mark sword_sheathed
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_drawn_held
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 3
mark sheathed_by_letting_go
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark drawn_again
right pos 0.00 -0.33 -0.17
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 3
mark stowed_at_chest
wait 1
