# The drawn weapon let go of at a holster and at the chest (game/WeaponRelease.h;
# free-weapons-spec section 5, decisions 3, 10 and 11): the sword drawn by the
# hold closing at the hip, held out in front, brought back to the hip and let
# go - sheathed, not dropped; drawn again, brought to the chest spot (0.17 m
# ahead, 0.33 m under the eyes) and let go - into the inventory, unequipped.
# The spot is set here: the player's INI carries Nadi's own placing of it
# (StowForward -0.08, StowUp -0.29 on 2026-11-20), and with it the let go at
# 0.17 m ahead fell beside the spot - dropped (failing since the spot was
# placed by hand, on every build OBVR bisected back to 2d22f2f).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-release-holster.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands StowAtBody=1
ini Hands StowRight=0
ini Hands StowForward=0.17
ini Hands StowUp=-0.33
ini Hands StowRadius=0.16
# The harness save's question box (Lovers with PK.esm active without its esp, since 2026-10-31) closed first.
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
expect Weapon hold: the hold closed on the drawn weapon
expect Weapon hold: let go at the holster - sheathed
# Hung back on its sheath as the ready click's Unequip begins, not in the let
# go's frame, where the engine drew it again first (Windows 2026-11-22).
expect Holster: the weapon hung back on its sheath - first person off the hand
reject the weapon NOT hung back
expect Weapon hold: let go at the chest - into the inventory
reject dropped where the hand is
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20
mark settle
wait 1
mark sword_sheathed
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_drawn_held
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 3
mark sheathed_by_letting_go
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark drawn_again
right pos 0.00 -0.33 -0.17
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 3
mark stowed_at_chest
wait 1
