# Leaning forward with [Body] KeepInView=1 (Nadi, 2026-10-28: since the body
# is kept in view, "nach vorne beugen ... geht der ganze körper bischen vor"):
# standing, then the head 30 cm forward and 20 cm lower, bowed 40 and 70
# degrees. "Body bounds:" gives Bip01 and the pelvis against the camera at each
# mark; the eye dumps show the body. Compare with body-lean-keep0.txt.
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\body-lean-keep1.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Body Visible=1
ini Body IK=1
ini Body KeepInView=1
console player.additem 0001C6D1 1
console player.equipitem 0001C6D1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Body bounds: at "lean70" - Bip01
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 14
right pos 0.30 -0.20 -0.45
right rot 50 0 0
left pos -0.30 -0.20 -0.45
left rot 50 0 0
head pitch -40
wait 1.5
mark stand40
dump
head pos 0 1.4 -0.3
wait 1.5
mark lean40
dump
head pitch -70
wait 1.5
mark lean70
dump
