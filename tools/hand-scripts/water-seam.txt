# Water reflections from the Talos Bridge over Lake Rumare (cow Tamriel 1 15 - the cell's centre lies on the bridge's line, y 63424) with the stable
# capture (WaterReflectionMode=2, the default): a Quest 3 tester's video of
# 2026-10-18 - across the lake one part of the water reflects the sky and
# the city, the rest is a flat colour, the two met by a straight seam that
# stands still in the world while the head turns. The capture camera faces
# the game's own heading (the pre-headset rotation, see
# camera::GetHeadIndependentWaterCameraTransforms) with a frustum three
# times the eye's across - about 76 degrees to each side; water seen past
# that samples the capture's edge, and past 90 degrees off its axis the
# capture's plane is behind it. The head is turned round the full circle in
# steps of 45 degrees, a picture at each: the seam should show once the
# head looks more than about 76 degrees off the game's heading, if that
# heading stays put under the turned head.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/water-seam.txt --run-timeout 300 --direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
console-at ready cow Tamriel 1 15

expect HandScript: finished
expect a world frame while the game loads
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 20
head pitch -15
wait 2
mark yaw-0
head yaw 45
wait 2
mark yaw-45
head yaw 90
wait 2
mark yaw-90
head yaw 135
wait 2
mark yaw-135
head yaw 180
wait 2
mark yaw-180
head yaw 225
wait 2
mark yaw-225
head yaw 270
wait 2
mark yaw-270
head yaw 315
wait 2
mark yaw-315
head yaw 0
wait 2
mark yaw-back
