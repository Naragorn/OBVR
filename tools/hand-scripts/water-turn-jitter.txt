# The water's reflection from one frame to the next while the head turns, at
# the Quest 3 tester's place (the rocky north shore of Lake Rumare by the
# Talos Bridge's west end: cow Tamriel 1 15 and player.setpos y 66400, as
# water-shore-tester.txt), clear weather, the stable capture on
# (WaterReflectionMode=2) and the reflection map at 1024 texels a side
# ([Render] WaterReflectionMapSize, render/WaterReflectionMap.h).
#
# What is wrong (Nadi in the headset 2026-10-22 and 2026-11-02, a tester's
# video 2026-10-21): the reflections "reset or come again" as the head turns
# sideways or up and down, and stand still when it stops. Read in the EXE:
# the engine's reflection map is 256 by 256 texels (push 0x100 at
# 0x0049BFAE), about ten eye pixels a texel in the headset, and the stable
# capture renders it anew from the head's rotation every frame - so the
# reflected shore's edges jump by whole texels with every small turn.
#
# The measurement (the game's clock slowed a hundredfold at the place,
# `sgtm 0.01`, so the waves all but stand - the first run of 15:28 with them
# running put 4 grey units of wave change into every pair of frames, still
# or turning alike, and the texel jump drowned in it): the head still for thirty frames, then turning a tenth of
# a degree a frame for thirty frames (`head spin`), the right eye written at
# every frame of both runs ([Debug] EyeDumpFrames). tools/water-turn-jitter.ps1
# reads the two runs: for every pair of frames in a row the best horizontal
# shift of the water box and the difference left after it - the waves and
# the texel jumps, with the turn's own motion taken out. Still against
# turning, and this run against water-turn-jitter-engine.txt (the engine's
# 256): the jumps are the turning run's excess over the still one, and the
# bigger map must shrink it.
# Run with: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\water-turn-jitter.txt -Direct
# Then:     powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\water-turn-jitter.ps1 -Run <the run's folder>

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
ini Render WaterReflectionMode=2
ini Render WaterReflectionMapSize=1024
ini Debug EyeDump=1
ini Debug EyeDumpFrames=30
ini Debug WaterCaptureTrace=1
console-at ready cow Tamriel 1 15
console-at placed player.setpos y 66400
console-at placed fw 38EEE
console-at placed sgtm 0.01
console-at end sgtm 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect a world frame while the game loads
expect Water reflection map: 1024 x 1024 texels asked for
expect Water reflection target: 1024 x 1024 texels
expect Water capture: the eyes look out to tangents
expect the right eye's frames in a row written up to still-f29
expect the right eye's frames in a row written up to turn-f29
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Water reflection map: WaterReflectionMapSize

wait 12
mark ready
wait 20
mark placed
wait 6
head pitch -15
head yaw 120
wait 3
mark still
wait 8
head spin 0.1
mark turn
wait 8
head spin 0
wait 1
mark end
