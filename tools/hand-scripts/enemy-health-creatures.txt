# The enemy health bars at a creature's own height (the tester, 2026-10-25:
# "ratten die nur am boden sind haben 2m über sich den healthbar"): a rat
# (CreatureRat 00025314) and a mudcrab (CreatureMudCrab 00000B7F) attack an
# untouchable player. Neither skeleton has "Bip01 Head" (read off the
# vanilla skeleton.nif files with pyffi), so each bar hangs over the
# highest of the creature's bones read now ("above its highest bone (no
# head bone)"), not a human head's height over the feet; the mark lines say
# how high over the feet (a rat's under 0.8 m). The console hurts the
# nearest at hurt1 and again at hurt2 (by then perhaps the other), so the
# bars come up.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/enemy-health-creatures.txt --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini HandHud EnemyHealthBars=1
ini HandHud EnemyHealthMetres=25
ini HandHud EnemyHealthAboveMetres=0.25
ini HandHud EnemyHealthShowSeconds=10
console-at ready player.placeatme 00025314 1
console-at ready player.placeatme 00000B7F 1
console-at ready tgm
console-at hurt1 {actor}.modav health -2
console-at hurt2 {actor}.modav health -2
console-at done killall

expect HandScript: finished
expect Enemy health: a bar over
expect above its highest bone (no head bone)
expect bar shown (
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject the overlay NOT up

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 1
head pitch -30
head face 1
wait 9
mark fight1
wait 1
mark hurt1
wait 3
mark hurt2
wait 3
mark seen1
dump
wait 1
mark done
wait 3
