# The feet at the ankle (docs/dismemberment-spec.md, "2026-11-16: the feet"; game/DismemberLogic.h

# its left foot thrown clear (mark "cut_foot_far") and its right foot where it lies ("cut_foot") - each

# shape's 8 bones: without [Hands] DismemberLimbSkin, on by default, nothing of it would lie there), the
