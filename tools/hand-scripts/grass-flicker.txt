# The grass frame to frame (Nadi in the headset, 2026-11-02, after the water
# fix: "grass flickert ... auch bei stillem kopf, eher nah vor mir ... eig
# alles gras"): what the grass does between frames in a row with the head
# still, and what the two eyes disagree on at the same frame. The meadow of
# the grass scenarios (cow Tamriel -13 21), clear weather, the head turned left
# to the grass patch by the path and pitched a little down (the run of 21:22 at yaw 0
# looked along a dirt path at a wall, the grass only at the picture's top left); both eyes at every frame of each run ([Debug]
# EyeDumpFrames), then a slow turn (`head spin 0.1`) and a big turn that
# makes the engine build its grass anew (game/GrassCone.h).
# Read with tools/water-turn-jitter.ps1 -Series still,turn,rebuild (the
# lower picture, the grass, frame against frame) and tools/eye-pair-diff.ps1
# (the left eye against the right at the same frame, the grass box against a
# ground box). The control grass-flicker-mono.txt (Stereo=none) says whether
# the frame-to-frame change is the grass's own (the wind, the alpha test) or
# the dual pass's (a time that differs between the two eyes' renders, as the
# torch's flame once did - render/BillboardTime).
# Run with: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\grass-flicker.txt -Direct
