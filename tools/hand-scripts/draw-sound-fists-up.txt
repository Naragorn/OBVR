# The draw's sound (game/DrawSound.h; Nadi, headset 2026-11-28: "manchmal keine equip sounds obwohl ich
# die ziehen konnte"). In that run's log the holster's "equipping the one kept there, then drawing" with
# the fists up (24275, 28480) put the axe on the hand with no draw animation - the engine plays its
# WPN...Equip only at the animation's Equip key, so that draw was silent. Now OBVR plays the engine's own
# sound for it, once, and says each draw and sheathe in a "Draw sound:" line.
# The claymore in the slot, the fists up (the weapon hand closed), then the grip over the right
# shoulder: the claymore equipped while out - OBVR's sound. Then the grip there again: sheathed by its
# animation (the engine's Unequip); and again: drawn by its animation (the engine's Equip, OBVR's none).
# The harness hears nothing: the lines say the engine's sound function was called (OBVR's own call, or
# the key's through the reroute), not that it was heard - 0x006B07F0 returns quietly with too many such
# sounds live or the actor out of range.
# Not run yet (written 2026-11-28 on Windows).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/draw-sound-fists-up.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands Fists=1
# The reach opens the hand after a draw: WeaponHold (2026-10-24) would drop it.
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.setav strength 100
console player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console player.equipitem 0001C6CD
expect HandScript: finished
expect Fist: the weapon hand closed - the sheathed weapon taken off for the fists
expect Holster: reach for the two-handed weapon - equipping the one kept there
expect Draw sound: 0001C6CD drawn equipped while out (no Equip group) - the engine's sound call 0 time(s) (0 kept quiet as a second), OBVR's own played
expect Draw sound: 0001C6CD sheathed by its animation - the engine's sound call 1 time(s)
expect Draw sound: 0001C6CD drawn by its animation - the engine's sound call 1 time(s)
reject Draw sound: the Equip call at
reject Draw sound: the Unequip call at
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
# The scripted weapon hand is an Index: its fist is its fingers (vr::FistFingersOf).
right index 1
wait 20
mark settle
wait 1
mark claymore_in_slot
# The fist: the sheathed claymore off, the fists up.
right curl 1
wait 2
mark fists_up
# The fist still closed, over the right shoulder: the claymore equipped while out.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
right curl 0
wait 3
mark claymore_on_hand
# Back over the shoulder: sheathed by its animation.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark sheathed
# And again: drawn by its animation.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn
wait 1
