# The grip trace ([Debug] GripTrace, vr/GripTrace.h): what a hand's grip
# reports - the pull, the force and the click - at its first tracked frame,
# every half second after, and at once when the click changes. A scripted
# hand's grip is its pull and its force alike, the click from 0.5 up
# (test/HandScriptPose.h): a light hold of 0.3 writes periodic lines without
# a click, 0.8 writes the click's line at once, letting go writes it again;
# the left hand, tracked and still, gets its first line and periodic ones.
# No "(not bound)" and no "(legacy)": the scripted hands report every action
# of the manifest bound.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/grip-trace.txt --run-timeout 120 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Grip trace: right pull 0.00 force 0.00 click 0 (first)
expect Grip trace: left pull 0.00 force 0.00 click 0 (first)
expect Grip trace: right pull 0.30 force 0.30 click 0 (every 0.5 s)
expect Grip trace: right pull 0.80 force 0.80 click 1 (click changed)
expect Grip trace: right pull 0.00 force 0.00 click 0 (click changed)
expect Grip trace: left pull 0.00 force 0.00 click 0 (every 0.5 s)
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject (not bound)
reject (legacy)

wait 15
mark loaded
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1.5
mark hands-still
right grip 0.3
wait 1.5
mark light-hold
right grip 0.8
wait 0.7
mark squeeze
right grip 0
wait 0.7
mark let-go
wait 1
