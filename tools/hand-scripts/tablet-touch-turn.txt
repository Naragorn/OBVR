# The tap's click as the finger turns back (Nadi's Windows run of
# 2026-11-27: "die Berührung auf dem Tablet kommt ein paar Frames zu spät";
# docs/inventory-tablet-spec.md 26.2). The finger on the Iron Longsword's
# row (tablet-touch.txt's place and geometry: a point d metres up the
# leant-back face at 0.0414 - 0.866 (0.377 - d) up and -0.4398 + 0.5
# (0.377 - d) back, h metres off the glass along its normal (0, 0.5, 0.866),
# the controller's tip 8 cm ahead; the row at d 0.26), in order:
# - hover 2.5 cm in front, then at the glass (the touch begins), then 1 cm
#   through it, 0.1 s each;
# - turned back by 8 mm, still 2 mm behind the glass, and kept there 0.2 s
#   (0.4 s on the glass in all, under the hold's 0.5 s): the tap's click
#   goes out here now;
# - then slid 25 cm to the side, off the face's edge: a touch still running
#   ends there with nothing clicked.
# With the DLL from before the click waited for 2 cm of the way out, so the
# touch was still running at the slide and ended "off the face's edge" with
# no tap (the reject below); with this change the tap comes at the turn and
# the sword is equipped.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/tablet-touch-turn.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1

ini Hands LeftHandGripZ=0
ini Hands HandGripUpMetres=0
ini Hands HandGripForwardMetres=0

ini Debug EyeDump=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Tablet: the Inventory page read
expect Tablet: at the mark the finger is over "Iron_Longsword"
count 1 Tablet: the finger tapped
expect where it went down - pulled back behind the glass
expect Holster: the Iron Longsword, equipped
reject nothing clicked - off the face's edge
reject HandScript: WARNING
