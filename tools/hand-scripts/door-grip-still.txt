# Doors by grip (vr/DoorGrip.h): what does not open a door. The plain door of
# door-grip-push.txt, half in the floor as there (its handle band at the
# floor); the right grip closes at its handle and stays there - no
# pull, no push - and opens again: taken, let go, never opened. Then a sword
# is drawn from the right hip and the LEFT hand closes its grip at the handle:
# with a weapon readied neither hand takes a door (as opening by reaching).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/door-grip-still.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands DoorsByGrip=1
ini Hands DoorGripMoveMetres=0.12
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready player.placeatme 0002318B 1 60 0
console-at ready player.additem 00000C0C 1
console-at ready player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Door: state - the nearest door
expect taken at the handle by the right hand
expect let go before it opened
expect Hands: ready weapon done
count 1 taken at the handle
reject opened by the grip, as A
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 10
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
mark ready
wait 4
mark placed
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.50
wait 0.5
right pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.66
wait 0.5
mark at
right grip 1
wait 2
mark held
right grip 0
wait 1
mark let
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 0.5
right pos 0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right pos 0.20 -0.45 -0.30
wait 2
mark drawn
left pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.50
wait 0.5
left pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.66
wait 0.5
left grip 1
wait 1
mark weapon
left pos -0.40 -1.60 -0.46
wait 0.5
left grip 0
wait 1
mark end
wait 1
