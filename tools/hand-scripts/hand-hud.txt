# The HUD on the hands (docs/hud-on-hands-spec.md): the bars, the spell, the
# weapon and the compass are found in the HUD's tile tree, lifted out of the
# captured interface into the atlas and erased from the panel. Writes the
# atlas and the panel left behind as pictures (HudTileProbe). The quads
# themselves need tracked controllers and a head pose, which a sleeping
# headset does not give - their placement is covered by hand_hud_test.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini HandHud Enabled=1
ini Debug HudTileProbe=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hand HUD: lifted out of the
expect Hand HUD: at "hud" - Bars left, lifted
expect Hand HUD: at "hud" - Spell left, lifted
expect Hand HUD: at "hud" - Weapon right, lifted
expect Hand HUD: at "hud" - Compass sky, lifted
expect Hand HUD: the atlas written, the panel left behind written
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 10
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 2
mark hud
wait 1
