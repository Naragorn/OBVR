# The right hand raised to the shoulder (Nadi, 2026-11-10, Windows: with the
# right hand at the shoulder a hand floated high up and far in front, above
# him). The right hand in front, at the shoulder, behind it, above the head
# and back in front; the left hand low at the side. At each mark the eye
# pictures (Debug EyeDump), the body's arms (Body IK at the mark) and the
# first-person hand bones against where the controllers put them ("Hand
# bones: at the mark"): every drawn hand must be at its controller, and
# nothing else hand-like in the pictures.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/hand-shoulder.txt --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug EyeDump=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hand bones: at the mark front - the right hand bone
expect Hand bones: at the mark shoulder - the right hand bone
expect Render: the eye pictures at the mark written
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
head pos 0 1.7 0
left pos -0.25 -0.55 -0.05
right pos 0.2 -0.3 -0.35
wait 1.5
mark front
right pos 0.2 -0.15 -0.15
wait 1
mark rising
right pos 0.2 -0.05 0.0
wait 1.5
mark shoulder
right pos 0.18 -0.05 0.12
wait 1.5
mark behind
right pos 0.2 0.15 -0.05
wait 1.5
mark above
head pitch -40
right pos 0.2 -0.05 0.0
wait 1.5
mark shoulder-look-down
head pitch 0
right pos 0.2 -0.3 -0.35
wait 1.5
mark front-again
wait 1
