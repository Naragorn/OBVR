# The hold on the drawn weapon read open for a moment (game/WeaponRelease.h,
# WeaponReleaseSeconds 0.03 since 2026-10-25): the sword drawn by the hold
# closing at the hip as in weapon-drop.txt, then the fingers open for 0.025 s
# and close again - one to three frames open, under the release time - so the
# sword stays in the hand and the log says the hold-on with how long it read
# open. Then a real opening drops it, so the release still works after.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-hold-flicker.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Debug GripTrace=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
expect Weapon hold: the hold closed on the drawn weapon
expect Weapon hold: the hold read open for
expect closed again - kept
expect Weapon hold: let go - dropped where the hand is - the new reference
expect Weapon hold: timeline - frames from the hold reading open: let go at
reject NOTHING dropped
reject let go at the holster
reject let go at the chest
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20
mark sword_sheathed
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_drawn_held
# 0.025, not less: the script carries a wait's leftover into the next, up to
# a frame, so 0.02 s ran out in the same frame as it began (2026-10-25 12:09,
# the grip trace never saw the 0) - 0.025 s shows one to three frames of it
# at 60 to 90 Hz, never 0.03 s of them.
right grip 0
wait 0.025
right grip 0.9
wait 2
mark still_held
right grip 0
wait 3
mark dropped
wait 1
