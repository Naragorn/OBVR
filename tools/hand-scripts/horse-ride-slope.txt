# The gallop up the priory's hill, for the saddle thrown on slopes (a Quest 3
# tester, beta 6, 2026-10-22: "whenever a horse starts moving uphill or downhill
# (tilting its body relative to the horizon), the camera gets 'thrown' out of
# the saddle. Once the horse returns to level ground, the camera snaps back"):
# horse-ride-gallop.txt with the horse put where the climb of 2026-10-20 10:43
# began (-54701 89209, heading 315 degrees - that run climbed 72, 235 and 71
# units over its three riding phases and the engine's camera fell to 45 units
# behind the horse's place in the yaw-only frame) instead of wherever it
# wandered to (the runs of 10:39, 10:50 and 2026-10-23 10:57 stood against the
# priory's buildings: no climb, nothing to read). The AI is turned off at the
# priory mark first, then the horse is placed and turned by the console, then
# the player is put 100 units south of it facing north for the mount. The
# The placement at the priory mark did not hold (2026-10-23 11:04 and 11:10:
# setpos on the horse with its AI running changed nothing, the heading took
# once and was turned back by the AI the other time - 308.8 degrees ran into a
# building, 315 climbed), so the horse is placed and turned again at the
# mounted mark, its AI off, where the 11:04 run stood when it climbed
# (-54581 89160), and the player is turned with it.
# The riding trace (Debug.HorseTurnProbe) carries, since 2026-10-23, the horse
# node's pitch and roll, the camera's offset in the node's full frame, the
# reference's rotX and rotY and the Bip01 bone's up shares - where the body's
# tilt lives, if it tilts, is what the climb's frames say.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/horse-ride-slope.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug HorseTurnProbe=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=0
console-at ready player.moveto 0002008C
console-at ready SetNumericINISetting "fAutoVanityModeDelay:Camera" 100000
console-at priory tai
console-at priory 0002008C.setpos x -54701
console-at priory 0002008C.setpos y 89209
console-at priory 0002008C.setpos z 13445
console-at priory 0002008C.setangle z 315
console-at priory player.moveto 0002008C 0 -100 0
console-at priory player.setangle z 0
console-at aimed tai
console-at mounted tai
console-at mounted 0002008C.setpos x -54581
console-at mounted 0002008C.setpos y 89160
console-at mounted 0002008C.setangle z 315
console-at mounted player.setangle z 315
console-at yaw-ahead tai

expect HandScript: finished
expect Camera: the camera node's ancestry
expect the player has mounted
expect the player has dismounted
expect Camera: riding at the mark
expect a world frame while the game loads
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 30
mark priory
wait 4

right pos 0.15 -0.35 -0.35
right rot -5 0 0

wait 3
