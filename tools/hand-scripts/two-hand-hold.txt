# two-hand-grip.txt with the left fingers closed round the handle but not
# squeezed (`left force 0`: the pull at 0.9, the force 0, no click): the
# left hand's hold by the pull (vr/GripHold.h) takes the claymore's handle,
# as an ordinary grip does, and opening the fingers lets go (Nadi,
# 2026-10-27: "ab normaler griff Stärke ... nicht nur auf 100 Prozent mit
# press so wie B&S").
# TwoHandReachUnits is widened for the run: where the scripted hands put the
# handle is not measured.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands TwoHandGrip=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands TwoHandReachUnits=60
console player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console player.equipitem 0001C6CD

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: the game's left hand on the two-hander read
expect Hands: the open left hand near the two-hander's handle takes its shape
expect Hands: the left hand took the two-hander's handle
expect Hands: the left hand drawn on the handle
expect Hands: the left hand slides along the handle with the trigger held
expect Hands: the left hand let go of the handle (the grip opened)
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Hands: grab - left grip

wait 20
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
# The weapon hand stays closed on the claymore: WeaponHold (2026-10-24)
# lets go of a drawn weapon when it opens.
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn
left pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.10
wait 0.5
left grip 1
left force 0
wait 1
mark both_hands
left pos 0.05 -0.35 -0.35
wait 1
mark turned
left trigger 1
left pos 0.20 -0.36 -0.02
wait 1
mark slid
left trigger 0
left grip 0
wait 1
mark let_go
