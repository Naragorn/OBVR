# A pull given up is no launch (Nadi, Windows 2026-11-27: pulled weapons "shot out past my hand"; free-weapons-spec,
# "Windows run of 2026-11-27"). The toggle off: the sword dropped at the feet, the weapon hand about a metre above
# it, the grip closed for 0.1 s and opened while the sword is still on its way - the grab lets go and the sword must
# stop where it was and fall (game/GrabPhysics.h ReleaseSpeedFor), not fly on with the pull's speed.
# Look at: "Hands: let go of ... stopped on its way in to the hand - the pull given up", and the next "Hands: flight
# of ... after the release" - its units/s per frame from about 0 rising with the fall, not 500-700 from the first
# frame as in his log (lines 9646, 10463, 27303).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-pull-given-up.txt --headless --no-steamvr --parallel --plugin build-linux-clangcl/OBVR.dll

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands GrabEquipsWeapons=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands PullToHand=1
ini Hands PullReachMetres=2.0
ini Hands GripToggle=0
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.drop 00000C0C 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect a grip pulls it to the hand
expect Hands: grab - grip open, the object let go
expect stopped on its way in to the hand - the pull given up
expect Hands: flight of
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject pulled to the hand and taken

wait 15
head pitch -50
right pos 0.05 -0.55 -0.70
right rot -80 0 0
wait 1.5
mark near
right grip 0.9
wait 0.1
right grip 0
mark opened
wait 3
mark fallen
