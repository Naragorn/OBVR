# The drawn weapon thrown (game/WeaponRelease.h; free-weapons-spec section 5,
# decision 10): as weapon-drop.txt, but the hand jumps forward in the frame
# the hold opens (the speed is the fastest of the eight frames before the
# opening and the opening's own; until 2026-10-25 the jump came 0.06 s after
# the opening, which only passed because the speed was then taken from the
# eight frames before the drop), so the dropped sword's body is sent off with
# the hand's speed when it arrives.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/weapon-throw.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: ready weapon done
expect Weapon hold: the hold closed on the drawn weapon
expect Weapon hold: let go - dropped where the hand is - the new reference
expect (a throw)
expect has its body
reject sent off at 0.00 m/s
reject NOTHING dropped
reject got no body
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 20

right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_drawn_held

right grip 0
wait 3
mark thrown
