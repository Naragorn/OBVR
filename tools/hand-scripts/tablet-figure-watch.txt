# The figure behind its tab, watched for flicker (Nadi's Windows run of
# 2026-11-27: "wenn ich den Reiter oben wähle, dann flackert die Figur im
# Tablet"; docs/inventory-tablet-spec.md 26.1). Not a pass or fail of the
# flicker itself: it collects what pins its cause.
# tablet-figure-tab.txt's opening and press on the figure's tab; then the
# tablet held still for 4 s (marks still-1, still-2), then the off hand
# turned by 2 degrees every 0.1 s for 2 s (mark turned).
# Look for:
# - "Tablet: the figure's rectangle as read over 90 frames - it moved in N
#   frame(s), by at most P layer pixels (held steady on the tablet); not
#   read in M frame(s)": said only when a read moved or failed. The reads
#   move with the figure's breath (read anew every 15 frames from its
#   bones); the tablet lays out the rectangle held steady
#   (vr/TabletFigureSteady.h), so the figure and its page's edge stand
#   still in the film while the hand is still. "not read": the tablet showed
#   the page alone in those frames.
# - The film still flickers while the hand is still, and P is over 34: the
#   steady rectangle's room is too small. Flickers only while the hand
#   turns: the figure's picture is sampled too small without mipmaps (its
#   hard edges crawl) - tools/film-flicker.py on the figure's part of the
#   film, still against turned.
