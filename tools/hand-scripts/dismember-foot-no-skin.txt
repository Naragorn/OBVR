# A foot is not cut with the limbs' own skin off (game/CutLimbsLogic.h CutShows): a foot is 2 of its
# shape's 8 bones, so without the copy drawn for the part it would collapse into the stump and nothing
# would lie on the ground - the cut is refused and says why. The lower leg, which has a shape of its own
# (the boot), is still cut with the setting off.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/dismember-foot-no-skin.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Dismemberment=1
ini Hands DismemberLimbSkin=0
ini Debug TestCut=1
ini Render MonitorMenusOnly=0
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setangle z 0
console-at placed player.placeatme 00069AD1 1 90 0
console-at kill killall

expect HandScript: finished
expect Dismember: test foot cut at the mark - nothing of it would be seen without the limbs' own skin
reject foot cut - 1 ankle joint(s) released
expect Dismember: test leg cut at the mark - cut
expect 's left lower leg cut - 1 knee joint(s) released
reject of the body's shape(s) drawn again for the part
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject CRASH

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 0.5
mark placed
wait 1
mark kill
wait 3
head pitch -55
wait 0.5
mark lying
wait 0.5
mark cut_foot
wait 1
mark cut_leg
wait 1.5
mark end
