# A real crouch and the camera's height (Nadi, 2026-10-24: "crouchen in real
# life [verursacht] auch einen kamera sprung nach unten. wegpatchen (default
# an)" - controls-spec 4.27). The head goes 45 cm down, past CrouchDropMetres
# (0.30): OBVR asks the game's sneak, and the eyes should fall by the head's
# 45 cm alone. The engine's eye above the feet is logged around each change
# of the sneak and at every mark ("Camera: the engine's eye ..."), the eyes'
# world height at every mark ("Camera: at the mark - the eyes at").
# [Hands] KeepEyeHeightSneaking (default 1) holds the engine's eye at its
# standing height while a real crouch sneaks (camera/SneakEyeHold.h); with it
# 0 the hold's lines are missing and the run fails. The stick's sneak while
# standing is the game's own since 2026-10-26 (Nadi: "sneaken mit button geht
# nun nicht mehr ... der player body bleibt stehend"): not held, the eyes and
# the body go down with it - unless the head crouches during it, then its
# drop is held from there.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/crouch-camera.txt --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands CrouchSneak=1
ini Body Visible=1
ini Body IK=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: crouched in the room - the game's sneak asked for
expect Hands: stood up in the room - the game's sneak to end
expect Camera: the engine's eye
count 1 Camera: the sneak lowers the engine's eye - held at the standing height
count 2 Camera: a sneak with the head standing (the stick's or a button's) - the game lowers the eye and the body, not held
count 1 Camera: the head crouched during the sneak - its drop held from now
count 2 Camera: the engine's eye is back at the standing height - the hold ends
reject Camera: the engine's eye stayed low after the sneak
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 10
mark stand
wait 1
mark stand_2
head pos 0 1.15 0
wait 0.2
mark down_02
wait 0.3
mark down_05
wait 0.5
mark down_1
wait 1
mark down_2
wait 2
mark down_4
head pos 0 1.6 0
wait 0.2
mark up_02
wait 0.3
mark up_05
wait 0.5
mark up_1
wait 1
mark up_2
wait 2
mark up_4
# Standing, the stick's sneak: a flick down sneaks, the next one ends it; the
# eyes go down with the game's sneak and come back up (not held).
right stick 0 -1
wait 0.15
right stick 0 0
wait 1
mark stick_sneak
right stick 0 -1
wait 0.15
right stick 0 0
wait 1.5
mark stick_up
# The stick's sneak again, then a real crouch during it: its drop is held
# from then ("the head crouched during the sneak"); standing up ends the
# sneak, held through the rise.
right stick 0 -1
wait 0.15
right stick 0 0
wait 1
mark stick_again
head pos 0 1.15 0
wait 1.5
mark crouch_in_stick_sneak
head pos 0 1.6 0
wait 2.5
mark up_after
