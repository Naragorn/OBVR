# Drawing by reaching, left-handed (Nadi in the headset, 2026-10-20 evening:
# "controls gespiegelt geht. aber waffe ziehen mit links nicht"): the
# weapon hand is the LEFT controller and every place is mirrored
# (HolsterOneHandSide etc. `auto`, left-handed-spec.md section 4) - the
# one-hander at the RIGHT hip, the two-hander and the staff over the LEFT
# shoulder, the bow behind the RIGHT shoulder, drawn by the other hand, the
# right controller. The poses are holster.txt's, holster-two-handed.txt's
# and holster-staff.txt's with x negated, "right"/"left" being the physical
# controllers. Every weapon class: the sword, the bow, the claymore, the
# staff. The "Holster:" lines must read as in the right-handed runs.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/holster-left-handed.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
# The reach opens the hand after a draw: WeaponHold (2026-10-24) would drop it.
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
ini Hands LeftHanded=1
ini Hands SwingLight=1000
# An Iron Longsword, an Iron Bow, an Iron Claymore and a staff (00000C0C,
# 00025231, 0001C6CD, 000912BE), the sword and the bow equipped once so
# OBVR has seen both, the bow last; the claymore and the staff come in by
# the console at their marks.
# Strong enough to carry it all: the harness save near its limit shows the
# tutorial's "more than you can carry" box over the world (2026-10-27).
console player.setav strength 100
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.additem 00025231 1
console player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console player.additem 000912BE 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C
console player.equipitem 00025231
console-at bow_still_drawn player.equipitem 0001C6CD
console-at claymore_sheathed player.equipitem 000912BE

expect HandScript: finished
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - equipping the one kept there
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - sheathing
expect Holster: reach for the bow - equipping the one kept there
expect - the bow is drawn - the quiver's (the weapon hand at
expect Holster: reach for the two-handed weapon - drawing
expect Holster: reach for the two-handed weapon - sheathing
expect Hands: the staff's draw sound (00029BB2) played
expect Hands: the staff's sheathe sound (00029BB3) played
expect Hands: ready weapon done
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
