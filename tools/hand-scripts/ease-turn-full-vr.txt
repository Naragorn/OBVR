# Ease the turn ([Look] SmoothTurning) is VR View's (Nadi, 2026-10-22: "es gibt
# eine setting in obvr smooth turning. ich denke die conflicted mit full vr
# mode smooth turn"): switched on in Full VR it is left out, and the stick's
# turn is not eased behind the stick. The log says so once; the stick still
# turns the player.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/ease-turn-full-vr.txt --run-timeout 150 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Look SnapTurning=0
ini Look SmoothTurning=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Look: Ease the turn is on but left out - Full VR's stick turns at its own speed
reject HandScript: WARNING

turned start turned 10

wait 16
mark start
right stick 1 0
wait 1
right stick 0 0
wait 0.5
mark turned
wait 1
