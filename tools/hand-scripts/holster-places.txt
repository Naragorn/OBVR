# The four places on the body (vr/Holster.h; free-weapons-spec 5.12; Nadi,
# 2026-10-27: "Waffen, schilde und torch kann ich nun überall holstern wie in
# B&S"). The harness save's Elven Long Sword, equipped and sheathed, is kept
# at the left hip by default. The weapon hand draws it there, carries it to
# the right hip and lets go - sheathed and kept at the right hip. The Iron
# Claymore (0001C6CD), equipped by the console, goes to the right shoulder by
# default; the weapon hand at the right hip takes the sword back (equipped,
# drawn). At the right shoulder with the sword drawn the sign says to put it
# away first. Then the save OBVRPlaces and its load: the places come back from
# the co-save, and the reach at the right shoulder takes the claymore. The
# run's OBVRPlaces.ess/.obse are to be deleted after the run.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/holster-places.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands HolsterLabels=1
# Strong enough to carry it all: the harness save near its limit shows the
# tutorial's "more than you can carry" box over the world (2026-10-27).
console player.setav strength 100
console player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console-at at_right_hip player.equipitem 0001C6CD
console-at sheathed_again save OBVRPlaces
console-at saved load OBVRPlaces

expect HandScript: finished
expect equipped and kept at no place, is kept at the left hip
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - drawing
expect let go at the right hip - sheathed and kept there
expect is kept at the right shoulder
expect Holster sign: at the right hip
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - equipping the one kept there, then drawing
expect "Put the weapon away first"
expect Holster: the places read from the co-save - 2 of 4 keep something
expect Holster: reach for the two-handed weapon - equipping the one kept there, then drawing
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject - "empty"
reject draw given up
reject never showed in the hand
reject dropped where the hand is

wait 20
mark ready
# Draw at the left hip, carry, let go at the right hip.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_drawn
right pos 0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.5
right grip 0
wait 3
mark at_right_hip
wait 3
mark claymore_equipped
# The sword back from the right hip: equipped, then drawn.
right pos 0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark sword_back
# At the back with the sword drawn: the sign says to put it away first.
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 1.5
mark sign_at_back
right pos 0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.5
right grip 0
wait 3
mark sheathed_again
wait 3
mark saved
wait 25
mark loaded
wait 3
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 0.9
wait 0.3
right pos 0.30 -0.40 -0.35
wait 3
mark claymore_drawn
wait 1
