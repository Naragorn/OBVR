# The counterpart of take-only-by-hand.txt, with TakeOnlyByHand off (the
# default): the same A press at the dropped Iron Longsword takes it at once,
# as vanilla's activate does - so what kept it on the floor there was the
# option, and the A was aimed at the sword. No mark before the press: the
# only items lines are from after it, and none may find the sword.
#
# This is the path of the crash of 2026-10-12 (a Steel Shield taken from the
# floor with A, the game crashed in OBVR): the pick's hold had the sword and
# kept it through its grace after the game deleted it, and the ring's settle
# read its base form through the old address. The hold has to let the taken
# thing go the frame it is gone from the cell ("Pick: ... is gone from the
# cell"), and nothing may read through it (CameraHook.cpp SettledRef,
# game/PickHold.h).
# Run with: tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\activate-takes.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands StowAtBody=1
ini Hands TakeOnlyByHand=0
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.drop 00000C0C 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect HandScript: mark after_activate
expect is gone from the cell (taken, deleted or hidden) - let go at once
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Hands: activate kept from the game
reject HandScript: items - near a hand 1

wait 10
head pitch -50
right pos 0.05 -1.40 -0.80
right rot -80 0 0
wait 1
right a 1
wait 0.3
right a 0
wait 1
mark after_activate
