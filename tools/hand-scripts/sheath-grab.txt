# The sheathed weapon drawn on the body is taken at its hilt, wherever the places are
# (vr/SheathReach.h, camera/hands/SheathGrab.cpp; Nadi, 2026-11-16: "immer greifbar ... egal wie der
# body ausgerichtet ist oder ob er nicht im holster spot ist").
# The longsword kept at the left hip, and the one-hander's place moved far off (HolsterOneHandUp to the
# knees), so no place is where the sword hangs. A grip at the hilt on the drawn body must draw it:
# "Sheath grab: the one-hander on the body hangs ..." names where it hangs in the body's frame and how
# far the gripping hand was, "drawing, taken at its hilt on the body" is the draw.
# NOT RUN YET (written 2026-11-16 on Windows with the harness off). The hilt's place is not known before
# the first run: the grips below try the left hip as the settings' default has it and two spots around
# it; take the "hangs" line of the first run and put the hand there if none of them is within 0.17 m.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/sheath-grab.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Body Visible=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
# The hips' places down at the knees: the hilt is in no place.
ini Hands HolsterOneHandUp=-1.10
ini Debug EyeDump=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.setav strength 100
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.equipitem 00000C0C
expect HandScript: finished
expect Sheath grab: the one-hander on the body hangs
expect drawing, taken at its hilt on the body
reject HandScript: WARNING
wait 20
mark settle
head pitch -50
wait 1
mark sword_on_body
# The left hip as the default place has it.
right pos -0.18 -0.62 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 1.5
mark grip_default_hip
# A hand's width lower and further out.
right pos -0.26 -0.72 -0.02
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 1.5
mark grip_lower_out
# And further back.
right pos -0.22 -0.66 0.10
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 2
mark end
