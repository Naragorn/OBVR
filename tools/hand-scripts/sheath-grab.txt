# The sheathed weapon drawn on the body is taken at its hilt, wherever the places are
# (vr/SheathReach.h, camera/hands/SheathGrab.cpp; Nadi, 2026-11-16: "immer greifbar ... egal wie der
# body ausgerichtet ist oder ob er nicht im holster spot ist").
# The harness save's Elven Longsword, sheathed at the left hip, and every place moved far off (down to
# the knees), so no place is where the sword hangs. A grip at the hilt on the drawn body must draw it:
# "Sheath grab: (mark ...) the one-hander on the body hangs ..." names where it hangs in the
# body's frame, "drawing, taken at its hilt on the body" is the draw.
# Measured 2026-11-16 (Linux, sheath-explore 20261116-094708): the one-hander's hilt hangs -0.19 right,
# 0.00 forward, -0.57 up of the eyes (the two-hander's -0.16 -0.16 -0.05, the bow's -0.03 -0.26 -0.38:
# sheath-grab-two-hander.txt, sheath-grab-bow.txt). A script's "pos" is right, up, back.
# First a grip 0.30 m from the hilt, which must draw nothing (the reach is 0.17 m).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/sheath-grab.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Body Visible=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=0
# Every place down at the knees: the hilt is in no place.
ini Hands HolsterOneHandUp=-1.10
ini Hands HolsterTwoHandUp=-1.10
ini Hands HolsterBowUp=-1.10
ini Debug EyeDump=1
console-at settle CloseAllMenus
console player.setav strength 100
expect HandScript: finished
expect Sheath grab: (mark sword_on_body) the one-hander on the body hangs
expect Holster: reach for the one-handed weapon - drawing, taken at its hilt on the body
count 1 taken at its hilt on the body
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject draw given up
wait 20
mark settle
head pitch -50
wait 1
mark sword_on_body
# 0.30 m out to the side of the hilt: not taken.
right pos -0.49 -0.57 0.00
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
wait 1.5
mark grip_beside
# At the hilt.
right pos -0.19 -0.57 0.00
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
right grip 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 2.5
mark drawn
wait 0.5
