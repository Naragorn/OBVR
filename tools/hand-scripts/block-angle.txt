# The block's angle and reach ([Hands] BlockByAngle, BlockReachUnits;
# game/BlockAngleLogic.h, the veto in game/BlockCone): a melee bandit
# (00069AD1) attacks an untouchable player (tgm - the hit handler still
# runs), the head facing it (head face 1), a longsword drawn by the holster
# and raised as a guard - first level across the body, then upright, then
# level again but held high over the head - each held half a minute. Every
# blow the engine would block is judged at the blow: "Block angle: a blow
# from ... at the weapon - the blades, N degrees (at least 60), M units off
# (at most 40) - blocked / THROUGH, the guard stands wrong / THROUGH, the
# blow passes the guard"; the state lines at the marks show the player
# blocking (the guard holds with the blade either way, since the rule
# decides at the blow). Which verdicts come depends on the bandit's swings;
# the run shows the angles and distances read. At the level guard the
# blades passed 16 to 37 units apart (2026-10-14); the high guard is
# expected to let blows through by the distance.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/block-angle.txt
#      or:  tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\block-angle.txt
