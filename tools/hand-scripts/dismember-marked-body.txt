# The parts cut off a body that was struck first (docs/dismemberment-spec.md, "2026-11-16: crash at a hand cut
# off a marked body"): every dismember scenario before this one cut a body with no mark on it, and the game
# crashed in the headset at the first hand cut off a corpse with cut marks and a blood stain on its skin - the
# engine's decal shapes were copied for the part, and the decal shader reads a copy's empty decal list.
# Here the body is marked as in a fight before each round of cuts, by the real blade (blade-entry.txt's fast
# stab and its two slashes across the chest; MotionHits=0, so no hit is handed to the game and the beggar
# lives), then killed, then cut by [Debug] TestCut at one part of every kind:
#   body 1, stripped (the marks on its skin):  the left hand, the right arm, the left lower leg, the right foot
#   body 2, in its clothes (the marks on them): the left forearm, the left foot, the head
# One cut a limb, so the kinds are spread over two bodies; the first is disabled before the second is placed.
# After each cut the world is drawn for 1.5 s (the crash came in the cut's own frame). Must be seen: marks and
# a stain laid on each body, every cut made, and at least one decal shape left out of a part's copies ("the
# decal shape ... is not drawn again for the part") - without that line no decal shape held a bone of a cut
# part and the run proved nothing. The eye pictures at the after_* marks show the parts on the ground.
# The sides TestCut has no mark for (the right hand and forearm, the left arm, the right lower leg) are not cut.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/dismember-marked-body.txt
