# The kick on the right B and the pause menu on the left B held
# (game/KickLogic.h, vr::StepButtonHold; the tester, 2026-10-25, Nadi
# 2026-10-30: "go B" - the foot seen, by the leg IK, walking not in its way).
# Seen: the right B kicks ("Kick: 1 - the right leg out"), the leg IK puts the
# foot ahead of the hip ("Body IK: the kick's right foot out"), the foot at
# the mark well above the feet - standing, and again while walking; the left
# B let go with the ring filling (1 s) calls it off; held, the ring over the
# controller from 0.5 s (its picture saved, OBVR-MenuHold-ring.bmp) opens the
# pause menu once full at 2 s; in the menu the
# right B closes it and kicks nothing; a short left B opens the menus on its
# release. The body's own view at the marks (Debug.BodyView): looking down
# (head pitch -55) the leg's share of the view grows from down_standing to
# down_kicked.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/kick.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Kick=1
ini Hands MenuHoldSeconds=2.0
ini Hands MenuRingSeconds=0.5
ini Debug BodyView=1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Kick: 1 - the right leg out
expect Body IK: the kick's right foot out
expect Kick: 2 - the right leg out
expect Hands: the left B held - the pause menu's ring shown over the controller
expect HandScript: the menu hold's ring saved as OBVR-MenuHold-ring.bmp
expect Hands: the left B held 2.00 s - the pause menu
expect Hands: the left B let go with the ring
count 3 Kick: at the mark the foot is
expect Kick: 3 - the right leg out
reject Kick: 4 - the right leg out
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark standing
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark kicked
right b 0
wait 1.5
mark back
left stick 0 1
wait 0.8
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark kicked_walking
right b 0
wait 0.6
left stick 0 0
wait 1
head pitch -55
wait 0.8
mark down_standing
right b 1
wait 0.15
mark down_kicked
right b 0
wait 1
head pitch 0
wait 0.5
left b 1
wait 1.0
left b 0
wait 1
mark called_off
left b 1
wait 1.2
mark ring
wait 1.1
left b 0
wait 1.5
mark pause_menu
right b 1
wait 0.1
right b 0
wait 1.5
mark closed
left b 1
wait 0.1
left b 0
wait 1.5
mark menus
right b 1
wait 0.1
right b 0
