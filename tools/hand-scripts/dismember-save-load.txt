# A head cut stays off over a save and its load (game/Dismember.h RedoCuts,
# DismemberLogic.h CutMemory, the co-save record OBCH; Nadi, 2026-10-31:
# "2 keine ahnung" - checked). dismember-corpse.txt's bandit, cut at the mark
# "cut" by [Debug] TestCut; the save OBVRCut, its load: the co-save names the
# head; the load comes from another cell (coc AnvilBenirusManor first), so the
# corpse's 3D is built anew - a load in the same cell kept it, 19:47 - and
# once the body's 3D has stood a second it is cut again, no blood
# and no message. The pictures after the load show the body without its head.
# Run with: powershell -File tools\hand-script-run.ps1 -Script tools\hand-scripts\dismember-save-load.txt -Direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Dismemberment=1
ini Debug TestCut=1
ini Render MonitorMenusOnly=0
ini Debug EyeDump=1
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setangle z 0
console-at placed player.placeatme 00069AD1 1 200 0
console-at kill killall
console-at cut_done save OBVRCut
console-at saved coc AnvilBenirusManor
console-at away load OBVRCut

expect HandScript: finished
expect 's head cut - 1 neck joint(s) released
expect Dismember: 1 head(s) cut read from the co-save
expect head cut again after its 3D came back (a load, the cell again) - off again
expect Dismember: a load since - 1 cut(s) dropped unread
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 0.5
mark placed
wait 1
mark kill
wait 3
head pitch -25
wait 0.5
mark cut
wait 2
mark cut_done
wait 3
mark saved
wait 20
mark away
wait 25
mark loaded
wait 3
mark after
