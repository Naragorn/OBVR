# Doors by grip ([Hands] DoorsByGrip, vr/DoorGrip.h, docs/door-grip-spec.md
# option B): a load door pulled open. The player is put before the load door
# of WarehouseFight (Oblivion.esm REFR 00025ED8, ChorrolLoadDoorMiddle02, at
# -251 49 -233 turned 180 degrees, read off the plugin 2026-11-11), 60 units
# off it and facing it; the empty right hand comes to it at handle height,
# its grip closes (a tick, "taken at the handle"), the hand comes back 18 cm
# and the door loads as A loads it - the player ends elsewhere.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless tools/hand-scripts/door-grip-load.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands DoorsByGrip=1
ini Hands DoorGripMoveMetres=0.12
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready coc WarehouseFight
console-at placed player.setpos x -200
console-at placed player.setpos y 49
console-at placed player.setpos z -300
console-at placed player.setangle z 270

expect HandScript: finished
expect Door: state - the nearest door
expect taken at the handle by the right hand
expect opened by the grip, as A
moved before after 300
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 10
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
mark ready
wait 8
mark placed
wait 4
mark there
right pos 0.00 -0.35 -0.40
