# The bow by hand (vr/Archery.h, game/BowVisual.h): the bow drawn from behind
# the left shoulder; the right hand's trigger over the right shoulder takes an
# arrow (it lies along that hand's laser), brings it to the bow (nocked), pulls
# back along the bow (the engine's draw), past a full draw (stopped there),
# eases back to the bow (the draw taken back - no shot), moves off the bow's
# line (the arrow off the string), puts it back at the quiver; then a second
# arrow is taken, drawn and loosed. At the end both sticks are held in for
# three seconds: the recenter.
# The runner takes two console lines at the start; the arrows come at marks,
# and a console at a mark leaves the menu state up for some seconds - the
# bow waits for it. The head looks down at the hands, and the eyes are dumped
# at the arrow's places: the game window's picture lags the eyes (2026-09-30),
# so the dumps are what to look at.
