# The bow by hand (vr/Archery.h): the bow drawn from behind the left
# shoulder, the right hand's grip over the right shoulder takes an arrow,
# brings it to the bow (nocked), pulls back (the engine's draw), lets go
# (loosed) - the shot along the line from the right hand through the bow.
# The runner takes two console lines at the start; the arrows come at marks,
# and a console at a mark leaves the menu state up for some seconds - the
# bow waits for it.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands BowByHand=1
console player.additem 00025231 1
console player.equipitem 00025231
console-at arrows player.additem 00017829 20
console-at arrows_on player.equipitem 00017829

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: bow by hand - an arrow taken from the quiver
expect Hands: bow by hand - the arrow nocked
expect Hands: bow by hand - drawn - the engine's draw begins
expect Hands: bow by hand - loosed
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 16
mark arrows
wait 3
mark arrows_on
wait 12
left pos -0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
left grip 1
wait 0.2
left grip 0
left pos -0.05 -0.10 -0.40
wait 3
mark bow_drawn
right pos 0.15 -0.10 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.3
mark arrow_taken
right pos -0.03 -0.10 -0.30
wait 0.4
mark nocked
right pos 0.0 -0.08 -0.02
wait 1.5
mark drawn
right grip 0
wait 1.5
mark loosed
