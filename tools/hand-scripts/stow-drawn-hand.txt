# Stowing measured at the drawn hand, not the controller (vr/Stow.h,
# DrawnHandInTracking; a tester, 2026-10-17: "cannot put a held item into the
# inventory, letting go at the chest drops it" - his hands adjusted to
# HandGripForwardMetres -0.23, so his drawn hand touched the chest while the
# controller stood 0.40 m out, outside the 0.16 m round the spot). stow.txt's
# sword, grabbed the same way; the controller then brought to 0.40 m ahead of
# the eyes at the chest's height with the hand drawn 0.23 m back from it,
# and let go there: the item is at the body and goes into the inventory.
# The spot and the hand's own grip are pinned to the defaults, so the run
# does not depend on the live INI's fit (run 1 of 2026-10-18 passed on the
# developer's fit - the hand 0.19 m behind the controller and the spot 0.27 m
# wide round -0.09 forward, -0.23 up - with the controller 0.50 m from the
# spot and the drawn hand 0.08 m from it). Measured at the controller, as
# before the fix, this script reads "away from the body" and drops the sword.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/stow-drawn-hand.txt --direct

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands StowAtBody=1
ini Hands HandGripForwardMetres=-0.23
ini Hands HandGripUpMetres=0.04
ini Hands RightHandGripX=0
ini Hands RightHandGripY=0
ini Hands RightHandGripZ=0
ini Hands StowRight=0
ini Hands StowForward=0.17
ini Hands StowUp=-0.33
ini Hands StowRadius=0.16
ini Render MirrorMenusToMonitor=1
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.drop 00000C0C 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Stow: the held item is at the body (the drawn hand at
expect Stow: let go at the body - taken (no owner
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject Stow: let go at the body - not taken
reject Stow: let go at the body - refused

wait 10
head pitch -50
right pos 0.05 -1.40 -0.80
right rot -80 0 0
wait 1
mark near
dump
right grip 1
wait 1.5
mark held
dump
# To the chest, the head level again: the controller pointing ahead, 0.40 m
# out and 0.33 m down, so the hand drawn 0.23 m behind it sits at the spot.
head pitch 0
right rot 0 0 0
right pos 0.02 -0.33 -0.40
wait 1
mark at_chest
right grip 0
wait 1
mark stowed
right pos 0.15 -0.25 -0.35
right rot 62 29 0
left b 1
wait 0.2
left b 0
wait 1.5
mark inventory
left b 1
wait 0.2
left b 0
wait 1
