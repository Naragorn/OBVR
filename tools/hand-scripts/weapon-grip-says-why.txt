# A closed grip at a weapon that it does not take says why (camera/hands/
# Grab.cpp, SayReachRefusal): weapon-pull.txt with the pull off, so the grip
# 0.23 m off the sword's grip neither pulls nor takes it - after a third of a
# second the log names the pick, what a grip does on it ("nothing yet") and
# the engine's pick. The tester's run of 2026-11-26 had five such grips at a
# sword on the floor and no line saying why.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --no-steamvr tools/hand-scripts/weapon-grip-says-why.txt

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands GrabEquipsWeapons=1
ini Hands GripByPull=1
ini Hands PullToHand=0
console player.additem 00000C0C 1
console player.drop 00000C0C 1

expect HandScript: finished
expect Hands: grab - the right grip reached
expect and the key stayed up - the pick
expect nothing yet), the engine's pick
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject pulled to the hand and taken

wait 15
head pitch -50
right pos 0.05 -1.31 -0.80
right rot -80 0 0
wait 1.5
mark near
right grip 0.9
wait 2
mark held
right grip 0
wait 1
