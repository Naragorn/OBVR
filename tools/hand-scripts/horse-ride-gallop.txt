# The gallop's bob (Nadi in the headset, 2026-10-19 evening: "the camera moves too
# much with the horse's motion"): the mount of horse-ride-steer.txt, then the left stick
# forward WITH the left stick's button held (the run), the head ahead, for eight seconds,
# then the walk for four. Debug.HorseTurnProbe=1 gives the per-frame riding trace (the
# engine's camera over the horse's place in its frame): the gallop's height, surge and
# sway against the walk's (steer run of 2026-10-20 09:44: 4.1 / 7.9 / 4.0 units, a
# 76-frame cycle).
# The AI goes off at the priory mark, as in horse-ride-turnprobe.txt, not with
# the move: held from the load (10:36) the horse stood as placed and the press
# mounted nothing, although the AI was on for it - the same way the mount never
# completed with the AI off on 2026-10-19. So the horse wanders for thirty
# seconds and faces where it likes (headings 2.8, 5.1 and 5.8 rad in three runs
# of 2026-10-20), and the gallop at 161 degrees ran into the priory's buildings
# after about two seconds; the free seconds are the measurement. At the end A
# again: the dismount, the riding base let go ("the player has dismounted" from
# the camera's lineage, the eyes at the dismounted mark on the player's feet).
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py tools/hand-scripts/horse-ride-gallop.txt --run-timeout 300 --headless

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug HorseTurnProbe=1
ini Hands ReachOpens=0
console-at ready player.moveto 0002008C
console-at ready SetNumericINISetting "fAutoVanityModeDelay:Camera" 100000
console-at priory player.moveto 0002008C 0 -100 0
console-at priory player.setangle z 0
console-at priory tai
console-at aimed tai
console-at mounted tai
console-at yaw-ahead tai

expect HandScript: finished
expect Camera: the camera node's ancestry
expect the player has mounted
expect the player has dismounted
expect Camera: riding at the mark
expect a world frame while the game loads
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 30
mark priory
wait 4
head yaw 0
head pitch -20
right pos 0.15 -0.35 -0.35
right rot -5 0 0
wait 2
mark aimed
right a 1
wait 0.3
right a 0
wait 3
mark pressed
head pitch 0
wait 12
mark mounted
wait 5
mark mounted-5
wait 5
mark mounted-10
head yaw 45
wait 3
mark yaw-left
head yaw -45
wait 3
mark yaw-right
head yaw 0
wait 2
mark yaw-ahead
left click 1
left stick 0 1
wait 4
mark gallop
wait 4
mark gallop-8
left click 0
wait 4
mark walk
left stick 0 0
wait 2
mark stopped
right a 1
wait 0.3
right a 0
wait 5
mark dismounted
