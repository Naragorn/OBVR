# Where the hand holds the weapon along it after a handover, through a save and a load
# (free-weapons-spec 5.16; vr/HandoverHoldSave.h, game/QuickKeyPages.cpp 'OBWH'): the Claymore
# handed to the left hand as in handover-two-hander.txt, held 12.7 units below its grip, and
# saved; then handed back to the right hand (the hold another one) and the save loaded. After
# the load the claymore is the left hand's again ('OBWS') and held where it was saved, not
# where the frames before the load had it, nor at its grip ("after the load the weapon is held
# where it was saved", the palm measured on the hold). The load's side switch is no handover:
# two "handed over" lines, not three. Before 2026-11-25 the co-save kept the side only.
# The hands apart before the load: the left one open (the claymore's after it), the right one
# closed far below the weapon; it opens after the load. Two takes of the handle, none after
# the load. The head looks down for the films.
# Run with: python3 tools/hand-script-run.py --headless --no-steamvr tools/hand-scripts/handover-cosave-hold.txt
ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands LeftHanded=0
ini Debug WeaponSide=0
ini Hands HandoverReachMetres=0.12
ini Hands Holsters=1
ini Hands WeaponHold=1
ini Hands TwoHandGrip=1
ini Hands TwoHandReachUnits=60
console-at ready CloseAllMenus
console-at ready player.additem 0001C6CD 1
console-at ready player.equipitem 0001C6CD
console-at handed save OBVRWeaponHold
console-at before_load load OBVRWeaponHold

expect HandScript: finished
count 2 Hands: the left hand took the
count 2 handed over - the sides switch, it stays equipped
count 2 Hands: the weapon handed over - the hand that took it
expect Hands: the hold along the weapon into the co-save -
expect Hands: the weapon's side read from the co-save - the left hand
expect Hands: the hold along the weapon read from the co-save -
expect Hands: the sides switched with the load - no handover
expect Hands: after the load the weapon is held where it was saved
count 3 Hands: the weapon held along it -
count 3 Hands: the weapon held along it, half a second on
reject HandScript: WARNING
reject OFF the hold
reject too far, a drop
reject Weapon hold: the dropped
reject taken at its grip
reject two-handed sword's blade (half-swording)

wait 12
right rot 0 0 0
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
head pitch -45
wait 16
right pos 0.18 -0.15 0.12
wait 0.3
right grip 1
wait 0.2
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 3
mark drawn
left pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.10
wait 0.5
left grip 1
wait 1
mark both_hands
right grip 0
wait 1
mark handed
# The save, then the claymore back to the right hand: the hold another one.
wait 6
mark saved
right pos 0.40 -0.45 -0.10
wait 1.5
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.25
wait 0.5
right grip 1
wait 1
mark both_again
left grip 0
wait 1
mark handed_back
# Apart before the load: the left hand, open, holds the claymore after it (a weapon drawn
# with the hand open stays until it closes); the right one, closed, is the other hand after
# it, far below the weapon - a closing there would take nothing ("closed off the handle").
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
right pos 0.20 -0.90 0.10
wait 1.5
mark before_load
wait 25
mark reloaded
right grip 0
wait 3
mark reloaded_later
